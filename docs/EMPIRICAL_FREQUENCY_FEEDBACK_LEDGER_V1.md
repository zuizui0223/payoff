## Why the Primula system is the nearest current PAYOFF analogue

The PAYOFF frequency layer requires relative performance to depend on population composition. The audited Toräng study is the only currently recovered sister-program anchor that directly places morph frequency into the selection process itself rather than merely documenting static opposing selection, environmental regime dependence, separability, or later frequency change.

The useful bounded statement is therefore:

> Real floral systems can exhibit context-dependent frequency-dependent selection generated jointly by mutualist and antagonist effects, including rare-morph advantage in some populations and years.

The following stronger statement is **not** currently licensed:

> PAYOFF's shared-versus-differentiated architecture feedback parameter `eta` has been empirically identified in *Primula farinosa*.

## Candidate re-analysis questions for Primula

Before any promotion beyond Lane A, inspect the primary data/design for whether the following can be reconstructed without post hoc relabeling:

```text
1. Are the two morphs biologically interpretable as an architecture pair rather than merely two trait values?
2. Is a common plant-fitness or selection scale available for both morphs?
3. Can a D-minus-S relative margin be reconstructed at multiple morph frequencies?
4. Are rare-morph limits or sufficiently extreme resident-frequency contexts present?
5. Are pollination and seed-predation contributions separately available as well as net fitness?
6. Can interior-frequency observations be separated prospectively from endpoint fitting?
7. Are year/population effects large enough that one global affine eta would be inappropriate?
```

A likely outcome is that the system remains an excellent Lane A analogue while failing the architecture mapping needed for Lane P. That negative adjudication would still be informative.

## Immediate conclusion

The literature-recovery stage has already found a real-world anchor for the *existence* of context-dependent frequency feedback in a mutualist-antagonist floral system, but it has **not** identified PAYOFF frequency feedback for a shared-versus-differentiated architecture pair.

This is a positive ecological plausibility result plus a preserved PAYOFF empirical claim ceiling.
