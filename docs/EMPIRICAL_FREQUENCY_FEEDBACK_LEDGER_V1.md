## Three different empirical questions

### Primula — ecological-mechanism proximity

The Primula series is currently the closest recovered ecological analogue to the SCH mutualist-antagonist setting:

```text
2006 -> experimental composition effect
2008 -> frequency-dependent pollination/seed-predation selection
2013 -> causal selection mosaic and long-term morph-frequency evolution.
```

The useful bounded statement is:

> Real floral systems can exhibit context-dependent frequency-dependent selection generated jointly by mutualist and antagonist effects, including rare-morph advantage in some populations and years.

### Dactylorhiza — game-layer falsification quality

The Dactylorhiza experiment gives five manipulated frequency settings at fixed density and multiple reproductive-success components. It is therefore the cleaner test of whether a minimal affine two-strategy frequency-response law is compatible across a broad observed range.

A preregistered no-refit analogue is:

```text
freeze observed-range secant at p=0.1 and p=0.9
hold out p=0.3,0.5,0.7
analyse pollinia removal, deposition and fruit set separately.
```

This is not reciprocal rare-invasion identification because neither observed endpoint is `p=0` or `p=1`.

### Arabidopsis halleri — context-switch and public-data readiness

The trichome system tests a different question: can frequency feedback itself depend on an ecological state variable while the focal alternatives remain fixed?

The reported mesocosm result is exactly of this form:

```text
specialist beetle present -> rare-morph advantage in reproductive outcomes
specialist beetle absent  -> no detected rare-morph advantage; glabrous reproduction higher regardless of frequency condition.
```

The Dryad record already identifies the original workbook and its field/mesocosm/README sheet structure. Exact frequency settings and numerical margins are deliberately left unresolved until the raw workbook is acquired and inspected.
