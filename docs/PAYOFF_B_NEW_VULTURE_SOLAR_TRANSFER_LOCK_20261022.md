## Scientific target and source lineage

The previously opened original Ciconia ciconia physical weather series
showed, in retrospective source stationary-day case versus nonmove
observations, a negative shortwave solar anomaly difference
(-17.10 W m^-2). This was discovered after many exploratory tests,
not as a preregistered primary ecological theory result.

The *unopened*, distinct taxon source chosen by full public Movebank
metadata census was Neophron percnopterus, DOI
10.5441/001/1.385gk270, Study ID 9651291, original GPS MD5
578c45678d402d055000ea14e754b6da. The paper's published
habitat/conservation results are prior art and will not be reanalysed
as a novel finding. Source-only GPS screen already reported 16 animal
IDs, 29 February-May GPS animal-years, 30 7-day-deduplicated
stationary-to-northward candidates from 18 animal-years.

The complete source-only at-risk gate was executed separately,
BEFORE this weather script. It found **1,859** original observed
3-day stationary risk-days, including **33** raw later-northward
labels, **1,454** two-day-stay labels, **232** other movement and
**140** right-censored observations. The planned NASA daily query
universe is exactly **192** original 0.5-degree grid×year×month
batches, of which **18** strata contain both cases and stays and
**19** case-labelled days lie in such overlap. Do not change these
thresholds or query batches after observing the weather.

environmental variable and lag rule as the stork: NASA POWER daily
surface ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN (W m^-2) for UTC stationary source day−1
minus the mean of days−2 through−8, contrasted between later
northward GPS labels and continuing stationary GPS labels in the
**same 0.5-degree grid×year×month**. Weight each stratum by
the original number of northward-labelled source days.

Prediction registered here (post-hoc with respect to storks but
prospective for vulture source weather): negative sign, as in the
stork physical source pattern. Also report 10m wind speed, V10M

favorable or not. No sign-based case selection or excluded cohort
