## Canonical V3 evidence ordering

The manuscript should present evidence in this order:

1. **Exact model geometry** — what follows mathematically from declared models.
2. **Preregistered natural analyses** — broad birds, wigeon and negative gates.
3. **Published mechanistic anchors** — mule deer, godwit, geese, redstart,
   flycatcher / interaction studies.
4. **Post-hoc exact extensions** — prediction–correction substitution, clearly
   labelled as derived after relevant empirical outcomes.
5. **Prospective tests** — internal phase estimate, actionability r, passive
   retention phi, control gain g, checkpoint-optimal response peak.

## Core claim that survives every boundary

> **Seasonal tracking has separable prediction and correction components, and
> information can become more accurate while its behavioral usefulness falls
> because correction opportunities are being lost. Individualized feedback can
> additionally be distinguished from a common timing programme by its joint
> mean-retention and variance-contraction signatures.**

## Stronger claim that remains prospective in nature

> **Animals repeatedly estimate signed seasonal phase error at route
> checkpoints and use that estimate to choose the direction and magnitude of
> downstream correction.**

## Transparency rule

The post-freeze theory may reorganize interpretation but may not:
- retune any frozen empirical gate;
- recode a prior null as support;
- estimate q, r, phi, g or D_eff by back-solving from a response used to test
  the same mechanism;
- replace the frozen V2 submission artifact without an explicit new manuscript
  version and post-freeze disclosure.
