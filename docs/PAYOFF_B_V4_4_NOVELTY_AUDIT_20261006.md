# PAYOFF-B V4.4 novelty audit — 2026-10-06

Status: **CURRENT NOVELTY BOUNDARY AFTER STAGEWISE AUDITS**

## Bottom line

V4.4 should not be sold as discovering:
- spatial phenology predictability along migration routes;
- migration-stage use of environmental information;
- migration-speed adjustment to spring green-up;
- stagewise change in relative timing along a migration route;
- the distinction between environmental information availability and biological
  use;
- feedforward versus feedback control;
- value of ecological information in variable environments.

Those components all have clear prior art.

The paper's contribution is narrower and more defensible:

> **separate environmental forecast opportunity from realized phenological
> adjustment on matched temporal and decision-loss scales, identify where the
> conversion remains unobserved in a multi-species bird system, and connect that
> gap to a sequential correction architecture anchored by individual-level
> signed compensation in mule deer.**

## Closest empirical prior art

### Kölzsch et al. 2015

Barnacle-geese work already:
- quantified correlation and proportionality of spring onset between
  consecutive migration stages;
- related environmental predictability to arrival timing relative to the local
  green wave;
- showed systematic stagewise change in arrival relative to spring, including
  overtaking the green wave.

Therefore V4.4 cannot claim novelty for:
- environmental correlation chains as migration information;
- stagewise relative-timing transformation itself;
- the broad prediction that more predictable routes can support closer
  phenological tracking.

### Amaral et al. 2025

The source dataset itself already establishes:
- population-front migration speed changes with green-up;
- earlier/faster green-up is associated with faster bird migration;
- migration-speed adjustment is insufficient to fully compensate arrival
  timing for spring shifts.

Therefore V4.4 cannot claim discovery of:
- bird speed adjustment to green-up;
- incomplete compensation by migration speed;
- biological transformation of timing across space in this dataset.

The new stagewise analyses are a **re-expression of this population-level
movement response on the signed phase scale required by the current theory**,
not a new discovery of speed adjustment.

### Robertson et al. 2024

Western Hemisphere analysis already shows that many migrants align more
strongly with climatological than current green-up.

V4.4 therefore cannot claim novelty for:
- current versus climatological environmental channels;
- limited flexibility of migration timing in response to current spring
  conditions.

## Closest theoretical prior art

### McNamara et al. 2011

Cue-optimum correlation, slope, environmental variance and timing loss are
already integrated theoretically.

### Bauer et al. 2020

Sequential migration-stage information acquisition and its timing consequences
are established.

### Bernhardt et al. 2020

Feedforward and feedback as complementary responses in fluctuating environments
are established.

### Usinowicz & O'Connor 2023

The fitness value of ecological information in variable environments is already
a general framework.

Therefore:
- G(t) itself is not a new value-of-information theorem;
- the fact that variability changes information value is not new;
- N(t)=r(t)G(t)-C(t) is a declared seasonal reduced-form specialization, not a
  universal new decision theorem.

## What is empirically distinctive in V4.4

Within the same eastern North American bird network, the analysis separates the
following quantities through time:

1. standardized source-destination coupling;
2. destination phenological variability;
3. marginal held-out forecast value of a temporally leading nonlocal signal;
4. temporal lead of that signal relative to arrival;
5. signed target-relative population arrival;
6. route-level forecast-value transfer to realized mismatch.

The observed combination is unusual and informative:
- destination variability increased;
- nonlocal marginal forecast value increased strongly;
- the environmental signal became earlier relative to target arrival;
- target green-up advanced while estimated arrival changed little;
- route-level gains in forecast value did not produce a bird-specific mismatch
  improvement beyond structural nulls.

The novelty is the **joint decomposition of environmental forecast opportunity
and its missing conversion to realized timing**, not any one component alone.

## Same-system stagewise bridge

The restricted 31-pair / 14-species subset adds a useful bridge:
- the same species' population front generally reaches the mapped source before
  the target;
- the source-to-target front interval shortened while the green-up interval
  changed little;
- the between-period source-stage relative-timing shift was only partly retained
  at the target stage.

But this is supporting evidence, not the novelty headline because:
- the subset is selected toward shorter distances and stronger late coupling;
- stagewise timing transformation is close to prior migration/green-wave work;
- arrival posterior precision differs strongly between periods;
- inverse-variance reweighting weakens the mean-transform contrast;
- phase-retention slopes are not mechanistically identifiable after
  measurement-error auditing.

Licensed role:
> same-system population-level bridge showing that downstream timing is not
> simply identical to source timing.

Not licensed role:
> discovery of individual feedback correction in birds.

## Individual-level correction anchor

Mule deer remain the strongest natural mechanism anchor because published and
reanalysed individual trajectories show:
- strong phase convergence;
- signed speed adjustment;
- signed stopover adjustment.

These findings are prior art in phenomenon but provide the clearest natural
anchor for the theoretical downstream-correction layer.

## Journal implication

### American Naturalist

Still the strongest first target.

Why:
- one conceptual question;
- exact reduced-model predictions;
- preregistered environmental contrast;
- unusually transparent posthoc diagnostic chain;
- same-system population-stage bridge;
- independent individual-level correction anchor;
- explicit prior-art boundaries.

### Ecology Letters / Nature Ecology & Evolution / PNAS

The current evidence remains short of a clean top-general result because no
single natural system jointly identifies:

    G(t)
    + biological access
    + retained actionability r(t)
    + behavioral response
    + phase outcome

at multiple ordered stages.

A direct same-system stagewise test of that full chain would materially raise
the ceiling.

## Current one-sentence novelty

> **Seasonal environmental information can become more valuable and earlier
> relative to a biological event without producing proportionate phenological
> adjustment; understanding seasonal tracking therefore requires separating
> forecast opportunity from the biological conversion of information into
> correction.**
