## Fixed secondary estimand before fitting

Question: does the early advantage of experimentally supplemented pollen persist through later acorn development? This is an example of stage-specific persistence of an experimentally supplemented service, not timing mismatch x buffer. Drought and other causes not separately identified.

- Exact source hash, seven-column schema, years 2018/2019. Map unambiguously named treatment labels only (control, pollen addition/supplementation or pollinated equivalents); if codes are opaque, log labels and HOLD for author-grounded mapping. No label inferred from acorn values.
- Observational unit is tagged shoot; paired analysis unit is tree-year (both arms with at least one observed shoot). Treat repeated trees as bootstrap clusters. No shot count as independent replication. Use same complete shoots at both stages: nonmissing TreeID/year/treatment/tag/flowers/acorns_june/acorns_final, strictly positive integer flowers, nonnegative integer acorn counts at each stage <= flowers. Do not replace missing with zero; report per-arm missing and zero-flower exclusions. Duplicate tree-year-arm-tag key, unexpected years or invalid observed counts cause HOLD. Report any final count greater than June as a chronology diagnostic; rates still refer to the original flower denominator and no survival inference is made.
- Sum flower and acorn counts within tree-year-arm; form June and September rates using common original flower denominator. Average these rates equally across paired tree-years. Estimate treatment-minus-control risk differences in percentage points at each stage, and their difference (final RD minus early RD). A negative change is attenuation on additive scale; positive early RD required before calling it attenuation of benefit. Report both endpoints and differences regardless of sign. Report ratios of mean rates and final minus early log RR only when both stage rates strictly positive. No pseudocounts. Neither contrast estimates individual survival conditional on June set.
- Primary secondary uncertainty: 10,000 nonparametric full-tree resamples, seed 20261125, retaining both years and both arms in each resampled cluster. Use percentile 95% intervals. Require >=30 distinct paired trees, both years represented by >=15 paired tree-years, finite rates in all draws for a reported ratio CI. Report year-stratified point estimates (descriptive, not independent replication). Complete-case estimand only: cannot assume attrition independent or equal weights imply population-level lifetime fitness. No significance-based model selection.
- Exact data outcome is first opened by a separate execution of the frozen script. No post-hoc treatment mapping from response, endpoint change, or primary theta promotion.
