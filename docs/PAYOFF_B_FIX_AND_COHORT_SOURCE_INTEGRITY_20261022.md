# PAYOFF-B original GPS quality and overlapping tracking-device identifiers (2026-10-22)

Two independent questions were resolved at source-audit level, neither a new ecological effect.

**Daily-centroid state error across 7,368 original source at-risk windows.**
Among 366 subsequently northward-labelled source windows, **133** (36.3%) had at least one original GPS fix outside the original 30km stationary radius during their previous three allegedly stationary days. Among 5,483 continuing-stay-labelled source windows, **829** (15.1%) had such an excursion. These source observations repeat across individuals and overlap in calendar time; this is differential measurement risk, not a species-general ecological causal estimate.

The strict original 3-day all-fix-stable weather sensitivity was executed separately in workflow https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/actions/runs/39784335141 and did NOT promote weather correlations into perceived cue use.

**Published stork paper cohort overlap.**
The 2016 juvenile/adult fall paper (122 reference deployments) and previously used 2018 adult spring/reproduction paper (36 reference deployments) shared ZERO exact animal, ring or deployment identifiers, but **35 tag-ID values**. Only two shared tag IDs had complete, comparable deployment on/off periods, and BOTH had overlapping periods. Other 33 had missing comparable periods. This does not prove either shared animals or independence. Hardware reuse versus ID namespace/source re-identification is unresolved; the cohorts MUST NOT be treated as confirmed independent. The 2016 reference has 66 juvenile and 56 adult rows and 46 records labelled deployment ended in death; these are prior-art outcome metadata, not independent survival-response estimates.
