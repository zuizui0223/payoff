# PAYOFF-B next natural experiment: ranked source admission after houbara negative recourse gate

Date: 2026-10-08
Status: **candidate-source screen only; no new biological analysis, causal result or fit**.

## Source constraint carried forward

PAYOFF-B's mechanistic claim requires **within the same individuals**:
- a timestamped cue observed before each *new* route-stage decision;
- the animal's response among genuinely feasible options (not an inferred
  maximum from realized speed or stay time);
- competing calendar + persistent individual + social group explanations;
- subsequent energy/intake and independent demographic payoff for claims
  about fitness;
- resource / partner phenology if claims concern synchrony and
  ecological-network coordination.

Missing any component defines the corresponding **claim ceiling**.

The first source aligns most directly with the **route-stage decision** the current
Burnside 2021 Zenodo workbook lacks. However, the original Frontiers paper
itself explicitly states that whether departure timing causes the chosen
stopover strategy, or vice versa, remains unresolved. It also says the
repeated-individual mixed model did not detect a significant departure-date
association given its small direct-migrant sample. Any reanalysis MUST
preserve that negative sensitivity and can only claim a new mechanism with
independently observed additional cue/fitness constructs.

## Ranked-source disposition after original-article verification

The whimbrel study is scientifically closer to an **action-policy choice** than the event-only Asian houbara Zenodo workbook, because direct-versus-stopover strategy was recorded and environmental conditions along the route were analyzed. But it is **not an independent causal checkpoint experiment**: the main candidate weather contrasts were already tested and were not significant; only three birds switched strategy and the authors' individual-random-term control weakened the calendar association.

The caribou study strongly collides with the old PAYOFF-B claim that arrival convergence or duration compensation is novel. Gurarie et al. observed that basic pattern at continental scale. Their proposed maternal-condition explanation is **hypothesized**, not a demonstration of a future-information controller or of the survival gain from adjusting speed.

The little bustard original study already modeled GPS-experienced 30 m microclimate refugia and resource NDVI; the source describes 47 birds / 67 breeding seasons, but breeding-season duration is not observed offspring demographic payoff. Shuert et al. describe 41 narwhals with contrasting routing patterns; their energy-minimization interpretation is not the same as directly measured energetic expenditure.

**Actual acquisition decision:** NONE of these candidates has a publicly verified joint cue-innovation + action-capacity + energetic-cost + individual-fitness dataset in the present PAYOFF repo. Whimbrel is the most promising *conditional on legitimate raw data access*, but does not warrant new modeling now. Keep every source as an explicit hold until source content, rights and a genuinely untested estimand pass an admission audit. A study with no new biological construct should not receive a new empirical paper merely because a regression can be run.

## Actionable acquisition checklist for highest-ranked source

Request an authorized **raw or event-level** sample with an immutable study
version or file digest, including:
- anonymized individual ID + repeated years + social group/flock when
  recorded, GPS fix timestamps and quality flags;
- stopover entry, stopover exit, actual choices and dates at *each
  environmentally meaningful checkpoint*, not just origin/destination;
- weather/temperature/photoperiod at each point, with retrospective versus
  actually measurable *before the next movement choice* clearly identified;
- remaining feasible arrival-date/speed/stay interval estimated
  independently of the observed choice, or a credible instrumental variable;
- activity/feeding/energetics and subsequent survival/breeding with
  offspring-stage timing/resource series if available;
- all preprocessing/exclusions and an outcome-independent codebook.

Under **event-only** data: analyze only historical timing or route-choice
prediction. Under **cue + checkpoint** data: test out-of-sample
cue-conditioned route changes against year-specific schedule, repeated ID,
fixed duration, shared-weather and group hypotheses. Under **cue +
checkpoint + independently measured feasible set + fitness**: only then
fit PAYOFF-B's unique actionability/fitness mechanism with suitable
prespecification.

## Publication relevance

This is a source request / future study-design ledger, not proof that a
high-impact, multi-species information–coordination mechanism exists.
The existing Paper 2 continues as a *theory-led, tightly bounded* model
with robust structural-null examples. Negative V7R, V8 and conditional
goose/houbara gates remain negative or unidentifiable; do not transform
absence of fit into confirmation that a rational optimal animal refused
an opportunity.
