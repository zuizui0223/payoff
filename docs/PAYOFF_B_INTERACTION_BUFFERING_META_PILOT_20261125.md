# Cross-interaction buffering and residual performance costs: pilot / prospective extraction design

2026-11-25. New exploratory evidence lane, independent from closed V5 and frozen Paper 2 manuscripts. This is a design for further extraction, **not** an outcome-blind preregistration of already inspected studies. No old threshold or negative outcome is reopened. No eBird or TinyFish used; all candidate underlying source provenance still requires review.

## 1. Why this is not another temporal propagation meta-analysis

Brlík et al. (published 27 October 2026, Nature Ecology & Evolution, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-026-03208-w) studied 2,324 individuals/83 species, including migration-duration compensation and non-breeding timing reset. V5 was already closed in PR #297 after this prior-art collision; preserve 9 effects/5 cohorts as provenance, do not run its H1/H2. Weir & Phillimore (2024, https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.17294) already distinguished buffers of timing, fitness cost, and demographic variation. Neither the existence of buffering nor the fact that timing need not imply fitness is novel.

The candidate question is narrower: **Across interacting seasonal systems, how much does an independently identified buffer attenuate the performance/fitness penalty of a timing perturbation, and when does apparent functional recovery coexist with residual cost?** Quantitative cross-system identification, not descriptive positive-only synthesis, is the novelty target; further literature verification is mandatory.

## 2. Keep three measurement layers separate

A. **Timing/overlap process.** Estimate day-for-day propagation only within compatible event and target definitions, e.g. signed resource-relative error before/after a defined correction stage. Calendar departure-arrival slopes are a different estimand. A slope below 1 is not alone evidence of adaptive or causal correction; noise, changing targets and selection are possible. Never convert correlations to day/day slopes or combine phase retention across unequal scales. Record early/late direction and common ecological lag.

B. **Functional opportunity and realized service.** Flowering/visitation overlap, total prey delivered, dietary quality and realized pollination are not interchangeable. Where same-year phenological distributions exist, a separately frozen descriptive counterfactual can compare observed overlap against a translated baseline window with fixed width/shape; observed overlap minus shift-only overlap isolates a distribution-shape component under that definition, not a causal mechanism. Avoid circularly predicting mathematical overlap from its own duration inputs and calling it validation; test independent realized encounter/service if available. Use fixed observation effort and keep zero interaction and no support separate.

C. **Performance/fitness.** Reproduction (seeds, fledglings), survival, recruitment, and body mass/condition are different endpoint strata; do not combine proxies as lifetime fitness. Select endpoints before opening new outcome data. A matching/feeding improvement with negative performance is a joint pattern; it is not necessarily the causal cost of compensating. Do not condition on a post-treatment achieved match to identify the cost of a mediator.

## 3. Primary candidate: independent buffer-by-perturbation interaction

Require factorial or defensibly identified comparison of a timing perturbation D (0=reference, 1=perturbed) and independently manipulated or independently measured pre-perturbation buffer B (0=less opportunity, 1=more opportunity). Require actual partner/resource phase (or verified exposure context) and positive-valued appropriate endpoint means W_db. The causal version requires randomization or justified design; keep observational moderation separate.

For a common positive response type and comparable endpoint, the log-scale interaction is

    theta = log(W_11 / W_01) - log(W_10 / W_00).

Positive theta attenuates a negative timing-perturbation effect when more buffer is available. Confirm direction and baseline effect separately; if timing perturbation is beneficial, do not relabel interaction as rescue. For count/continuous means and risk endpoints keep likelihood and strata distinct. A joint model coefficient with uncertainty or the full four-arm covariance is required; no assumed independent arms for repeated units/shared controls. For independent arms only, a delta-method sampling variance sums Var(W_db)/W_db^2. Handle true zeros with an appropriate count/binomial model, not a post-hoc pseudocount. Preserve original denominators, randomization unit, treatment compliance, censoring, and attrition. No pooling of days, percent, standardized slopes, survival probabilities, or source-model intercepts.

For a secondary same-unit dual-response analysis, record both functional response and fitness/performance log response ratios under the same perturbation, with joint covariance or raw data for paired estimation. Do not subtract dissimilar endpoints or interpret their difference causally. Model cross-study associations only with enough independent cohorts. Keep a direct independent-cost contrast separate.

## 4. Initial data ledger and strict admission result

- [Source registry](../data/payoff_b_interaction_buffering_source_registry_20261125.csv): 18 study/synthesis records, 11 with archive or repository evidence links. Includes 13 primary-study candidates/anchors and 5 prior-art/sampling-frame records. This is a targeted seed screen, not a PRISMA-complete systematic search.
- [Published pilot](../data/payoff_b_interaction_buffering_published_pilot_20261125.json): 16 endpoint records across five paper lineages, 15 numerical, 8 with printed SE, one additional repository bootstrap interval, one not estimable. No primary theta and no pooled result. These are secondary published extractions or existing PAYOFF results.
- The strongest direct candidate pattern is Nanke et al. (2026, Oikos https://doi.org/10.1002/oik.12544): delayed pollination is partly accommodated by flower longevity but seed production falls 22%; warming separately reduces longevity 8%. The factorial warming × pollination experiment does not manipulate only correction opportunity; the 22% point cannot identify theta, and the original workbook was not opened.
- Samplonius et al. (2016, JAE https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.12554) provides paired source tables on diet and nestling condition. Source-standardized quadratic match association: caterpillar feeding -0.345 ±0.103, flying insects +0.142 ±0.054, spiders +0.104 ±0.045 (habitat interaction present). Day-7 mass -0.354 ±0.104, tarsus -0.091 ±0.034, eighth primary feather -0.852 ±0.294. Higher older-chick flying-insect feeding associated with lower day-12 mass (-0.433 ±0.121); younger-chick spider feeding associated with higher later tarsus (+0.095 ±0.043). These are model-selected adjusted associations, same source, not eight independent studies. Total provisioning term absent for match in selected model is not an equivalence estimate. Do not equate prey count with nutrition.
- Dossman et al. (2023) reports migration rate +43% and apparent annual survival -6.3% for a ten-day delay. Survival scale and causal speed effects need source audit. Bontekoe et al. (2023) delayed storks have reduced migration propensity (15/32) and different destinations, yet lower mortality in the published study: retain this counterexample; do not infer a universal penalty.
- Tree-swallow 2017 delay × brood-size manipulation is a genuine factorial candidate, but brood-size relief changes demand rather than purely recourse, and arm-level outcomes and resource-relative phase are unverified. A reported main effect does not identify the interaction.
- Conklin original archive is CC BY 4.0, file Conklin_GeoTiming.txt provider MD5 baafc80803a54da283604773748c1817. Direct file download failed in this session. No individual values or fitted slopes invented.

**Gate verdict: HOLD for cross-system inferential meta-analysis, 0 verified eligible primary factorial theta estimates.** This pilot establishes existence of source candidates and different outcome layers, not an effect size or the universality of compensation cost.

## 5. Systematic expansion and analysis plan (prospective, no new outcomes)

1. Start with the existing Kharouba & Wolkovich 2023 OSF source list (https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/AEYT2, 20 studies/26 interactions) and Loughnan 2024 KNB (https://doi.org/10.5063/F12J69B2, 1,279 time series). Use as sampling frames, not independent replications; most latter records are single dates, not joint process+fitness.
2. Screen the references of Weir & Phillimore 2024 and searches combining ("phenological mismatch" OR "seasonal asynchrony" OR "timing perturbation") AND (buffer* OR compensat* OR "flower longevity" OR "diet switching" OR "incubation adjustment") AND (experiment* OR survival OR reproduction OR fitness). Record databases, dates, complete returned identities, duplicate clusters, all exclusions and missing data. Include null and beneficial outcomes; no significance-based admission. Include mutualistic and antagonistic systems; split migration-only calendar anchors from partner-relative data.
3. Acquire original authenticated data first for Nanke (Data_Corydalis.xlsx), Samplonius (201213all.csv; feeding1213.csv; fitndiet1213.csv + R code), van Dis (CatFood2021_deposit.csv), Hall (phenological distribution and synchrony files) and the 2023 OSF sources. Verify exact checksums/permissions, schemas, shared IDs, time order, source/overlap policy; no eBird inputs. Preserve failed access receipts. Evaluate TreeSwallow factorial arm availability rather than extracting a convenient coefficient.
4. Define a pre-outcome primary endpoint and sampling variance within each admitted experiment. Require design/covariance, common effect metric and independence. Freeze a new extraction/synthesis plan before further outcomes. Operational initial threshold for a pooled exploratory random-effects estimate: at least 10 independent compatible study/cohort clusters, at least two biological systems for a cross-system claim, with moderator analysis only if within-class support is adequate. Do not relax on seeing effects. Report descriptive results if gate fails; avoid unreliable multivariate estimates with absent covariance. Use study/experiment clusters and overlap ledger, phylogenetic/species structure where identified, leave-one-study-out checks, heterogeneity and prediction intervals, early/late and observational/experimental sensitivity strata.
5. Integrate with Paper 2 only after distinct novelty and new data pass. A failed joint mechanism test or inaccessible source is not a negative biological effect. Existing V5, V7, E6/E7, prior drafts and frozen hypotheses remain unchanged.
