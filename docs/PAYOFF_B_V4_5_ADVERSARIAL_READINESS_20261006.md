## Reviewer attack 1 — "Your main result is posthoc"

Correct.

Only the delta-rho environmental contrast was preregistered.

The following were opened after the primary result:
- metric-scale decomposition;
- G_CV;
- baseline and source-rank sensitivities;
- observability audit;
- signed timing decomposition;
- G_CV transfer;
- stagewise population analysis.

Required response:
- label all of these posthoc every time evidence status matters;
- never call G_CV a preregistered success;
- preserve the failed registered degradation prediction prominently;
- frame the posthoc chain as diagnosis of why rho alone was insufficient.

This is a transparency strength only if the manuscript never blurs the order.

## Reviewer attack 2 — "G_CV is not biological information"

Correct.

G_CV is:
- an analyst/model comparison;
- finite-sample;
- baseline-dependent;
- capable of being negative;
- reconstructed from annual source mid-green-up.

It is not:
- organismal fitness value of information;
- cue perception;
- a biological information channel.

V4.5 resolves this by separating:
    G_E -> G_O -> r -> correction.

Do not revert to "information increased" without a modifier.

## Reviewer attack 3 — "The source event is not available to the bird"

Often correct.

Restricted stage subset:
- source mid-green-up before source-front arrival:
  29.8% early / 45.8% late;
- after target-front arrival:
  41.6% early / 36.1% late.

Therefore:
- "temporally available signal" is prohibited;
- "target-preceding environmental predictor" is only an average/order statement;
- "retrospective environmental forecastability" is safest.

The Amaral source paper supports local phenology-responsive movement, but does
not establish that the reconstructed source mid-green-up variable was used as
the cue.

## Reviewer attack 4 — "Two eight-year windows are not climate change"

Correct.

The paper may report period contrasts.

It may not claim:
- monotonic long-term change;
- anthropogenic attribution;
- climate change caused stronger forecastability.

No ERA5 extension is required for the current paper because attribution is no
longer the headline.

## Reviewer attack 5 — "Short windows make cross-validation unstable"

Partly addressed.

G_CV robustness:
- median and trimmed mean positive;
- 139/166 pair changes positive;
- equal-species positive;
- exact 8/8-year subset positive;
- 16/16 calendar-year omissions positive;
- alternative no-source baseline positive;
- source ranks 1/2/3 positive.

The result is therefore not carried by one year, one species weighting, one
baseline or one frozen source choice.

Still:
- G_CV magnitude should not be treated as a stable natural parameter;
- the direction of the population-level period contrast is stronger than any
  individual pair estimate.

## Reviewer attack 6 — "Nearest lower-latitude source is arbitrary"

## Reviewer attack 7 — "Bird arrival did adjust; your 'rigid' wording is too strong"

Current data:
- target green-up shift -2.31 d;
- arrival shift -0.19 d with CI spanning zero;
- signed relative timing shift +2.12 d.

Preferred wording:
**comparatively limited population arrival shift**.

Avoid:
- completely rigid;
- no adjustment;
- birds failed to respond.

The original Amaral study already establishes migration-speed response to
green-up.

## Reviewer attack 8 — "Absolute mismatch got smaller, so tracking improved"

Not necessarily.

Birds were several days earlier than mid-green-up in both periods.
Target green-up advanced toward the largely unchanged arrival front.

Therefore:
- absolute gap stability/decline does not identify active tracking;
- zero lag is not established as the fitness optimum;
- signed timing is required for interpretation.

## Reviewer attack 9 — "Your same-system bird correction result is measurement artefact"

The strong mechanistic version is not licensed.

Stagewise subset:
- 31 pairs / 14 species;
- selected toward shorter distances and higher late coupling;
- arrival posterior SD differs sharply by period;
- posterior-normal propagation preserves mean direction;
- inverse-variance weighting interval crosses zero;
- retention slopes fail errors-in-variables identifiability.

Licensed:
population-level stage transformation.

Not licensed:
individual feedback gain.

Keep stagewise bird result supporting, not headline.

## Reviewer attack 10 — "Mule-deer correction is already published"

PAYOFF-B contribution:

Do not claim discovery.

## Reviewer attack 11 — "The theory is standard decision/control theory"

Largely correct.

Novelty is not a generic theorem.

The reduced equations are valuable because they enforce the measurement

    forecastability -> access -> actionability -> correction.

The exponential t* result is a transparent seasonal specialization.

## Reviewer attack 12 — "Why is this one paper rather than a Perspective?"

Reason to retain Major Article:
- preregistered environmental result exists;
- posthoc diagnostics produce a substantive multi-species empirical pattern;
- restricted same-system stage analysis provides a bridge;
- individual-level natural correction anchor exists;

- the strongest biological mechanism is cross-system;
- accessible information and actionability are not directly measured in birds.

Therefore American Naturalist Major Article is credible but should be written
with synthesis-level conceptual discipline.
