# PAYOFF-B V3 reviewer-risk audit — 2026-10-03

Status: **scope-locked development audit; no new scientific claim**

## Purpose

Identify the most likely reviewer objections to
\`manuscript/PAYOFF_B_INFORMATION_CONTROL_V3_POSTFREEZE.md\` and verify that
the manuscript either addresses them directly or keeps them outside the claim
ceiling.

## Risk 1 — "Two clocks" sounds more empirically identified than it is

### Objection

The manuscript distinguishes a developmental/physiological entry timer from an
information-dependent decision controller, but no natural system jointly
identifies all primitive variables.

### Current defense

- title uses **can convert**, not "causes";
- Introduction says natural evidence is deliberately layered;
- mule deer are labelled T3_CANDIDATE + D2 -> H1_CANDIDATE;
- H2 IFBFat moderation is explicitly null;
- pure entry-only handoff is explicitly not supported;
- direct natural validation remains prospective.

### Claim ceiling

Use "timing architecture", "candidate hybrid" and "functional controller".

Do not write:
- "we identified two clocks in mule deer";
- "IFBFat is the readiness gate";
- "animals compute Bayesian phase estimates".

## Risk 2 — decision controller may be mistaken for a literal neural clock

### Objection

A feedback policy is not a molecular/circadian clock.

### Current defense

The formal text separates:
- developmental/physiological **timer**;
- inferential **decision controller**.

"Mikawa-Anjo clock" is explicitly informal and reserved for communication.

### Editorial rule

Prefer "entry timer + post-entry controller" in formal mechanistic paragraphs.
Use "two-clock architecture" only as the umbrella conceptual label.

## Risk 3 — mule-deer source data are used repeatedly

### Objection

Multiple post-freeze analyses of one source dataset can look like mechanism
fishing.

### Current defense

Each additional analysis has a frozen contract before its focal outcome:
- phase funnel;
- readiness temporal-order gate;
- channel dissociation;
- H2 proxy moderation;
- serial handoff.

Negative outcomes are retained:
- H2 proxy = NO SUPPORT;
- pure Markov handoff = NOT COMPATIBLE.

### Claim ceiling

Mule deer are a **mechanistic anchor / candidate hybrid**, not independent
replications of one theorem.

Do not count multiple analyses from this dataset as multiple empirical systems.

## Risk 4 — serial linear theory is too simple

### Objection

\(\Delta_n\) decomposition assumes homogeneous linear retention and can omit
new environmental innovation, persistent physiological state and nonlinear
control.

### Current defense

- main theorem is presented as exact for the declared reduced model;
- process-noise floor is explicit;
- persistent-state extension is SI/post-hoc;
- route-wise state model permits target innovation;
- no universal natural \(\lambda\) interpretation is claimed.

### Claim ceiling

Call the exact equations **reduced-model identities**, not universal laws.

## Risk 5 — clock portfolio is a stylized cost model

### Objection

Quadratic costs and \(n^2\) checkpoint scaling are arbitrary.

### Current defense

- fixed-capacity and additive per-use cost models are both shown;
- the exponent changes from \(n^2\) to \(n\), while the directional shift
  toward feedback remains;
- opportunity-loss fragility has a cost-independent share identity;
- portfolio is Discussion/SI, not Abstract spine.

### Claim ceiling

Do not call \(n^2\) a biological scaling law.

## Risk 6 — "portfolio" conflicts with the ecological portfolio effect

### Objection

Portfolio effect already means diversity-driven aggregate stability.

### Current defense

Novelty note distinguishes **clock portfolio** from ecological portfolio effect.

### Editorial rule

Always qualify with "clock portfolio" or "timing-control portfolio".

## Risk 7 — opportunity loss is hard to identify

### Objection

Loss of a named stopover is not equivalent to loss of useful correction
capacity, and population changes can reflect selection.

### Current defense

- #281 identification triangle separates loss, substitution and selection;
- natural direct-test lane is closed fail-closed;
- weighted opportunity uses historical precision contribution rather than site
  count;
- no current system is called validation.

### Claim ceiling

Use piping plover, Filsø goose and great knot as separate **anchors**, not tests.

## Risk 8 — interaction mismatch may be driven by ordinary differential
phenological sensitivity, making the control language unnecessary

### Objection

Why not simply fit species-specific temperature slopes?

### Current defense

The reduced theory distinguishes mechanisms that can yield the same final
slope:
- different entry phase;
- different information quality;
- different retained opportunity;
- different feedback gain;
- persistent physiological carry-over.

The pairwise controller identity shows how common forcing is transformed into
differential phase under heterogeneous retention.

### Main ecological answer

Phenological sensitivity is an outcome coordinate; the model decomposes how
that coordinate can arise and why historically similar timing can have
different robustness.

## Risk 9 — actionability theory and two-clock theory look like separate papers

### Objection

The information/actionability result and the serial clock result may appear
loosely joined.

### Current bridge

The entry timer determines the phase state at behavioral entry.  After entry,
information becomes more accurate while opportunity can decline; the decision
controller acts within that shrinking action space.

The conceptual sequence is:

\[
\text{entry state}
\rightarrow
\text{information acquisition}
\rightarrow
\text{actionable correction}
\rightarrow
\text{retained phase}
\rightarrow
\text{interaction mismatch}.
\]

### Editorial rule

Do not expand the separate mathematics in Discussion.  Keep the bridge verbal
and mechanistic.

## Risk 10 — game-theory coordination feels bolted on

### Objection

Why is equilibrium persistence needed after a control-theory mismatch model?

### Current role

The controller explains **generation** of mismatch.

The coordination game explains one route to **persistence/recovery failure**
when a physical correction remains possible but unilateral change is costly.

### Editorial rule

Keep game theory after the controller result and avoid giving it equal space in
the Abstract.

## Risk 11 — cross-system evidence is a collage

### Objection

Birds, mule deer, geese and plant-pollinator systems do not form one direct
test.

### Current defense

Evidence classes are explicit:
- preregistered analysis;
- source-data reanalysis;
- published anchor;
- post-hoc theory;
- prospective prediction.

### Claim ceiling

Never use "validated across taxa".
Use "independent systems support separate ingredients".

## Risk 12 — novelty is too broad

### Prior art that must stay outside novelty

- optimal migration and dynamic programming;
- stopover-based updating;
- partial-information control;
- anticipatory versus responsive plasticity;
- multiple physiological timing mechanisms;
- migration-network critical nodes;
- ecological portfolio effects;
- generic hidden/cryptic variation.

### Narrow novelty that survives

1. information gain versus shrinking actionability in one timing problem;
2. exact separation of entry-state and retained-error contributions in the
   declared serial model;
3. exact mapping from hidden timing-control portfolios to opportunity-loss
   fragility;
4. explicit fail-closed identification rules separating effective opportunity,
   substitution and selection.

## Publication recommendation

The V3 manuscript is strongest when read as:

> **a mechanistic synthesis of how seasonal timing errors are created,
> corrected and exposed by environmental change, with exact reduced-model
> predictions and deliberately modular natural evidence.**

It is weakest if presented as:
- a universal new biological-clock theory;
- a direct natural validation of latent control variables;
- a comprehensive theory of migration-network resilience.
