# PAYOFF-B: original randomized feeding-time intervention — source-grain gate

**Date 2026-11-20. Source-only prefit contract.** No previously published treatment outcome is refit or framed as newly discovered. The aim is to check whether the original experimental manipulation separates a *remaining physiological capacity* from mere availability of environmental information, and whether its treatment allocation provides independent experimental units.

## Primary original publication

Lameris et al. (2025), *Functional Ecology* 39:3434–3445, [DOI 10.1111/1365-2435.70183](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.70183), published 6 November 2025; original public data/code [Figshare article 28660718](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28660718). Source ecology: wintering Red Knot `Calidris canutus islandica` in the Wadden Sea, body-store deposition and pre-nuptial moult manipulated through **6, 12, or 24 h/day food access**, then birds released and tracked for migration departure. **Experimental earlier departure under additional food access is ALREADY PUBLISHED prior art.**

As reported in the original Methods, three treatments were **assigned by aviary**, with **one aviary/treatment in 2021** (pilot, **no birds tracked on release**) and **two aviaries/treatment in 2022 and 2023**. Birds were allocated randomly to treatments and aviaries, but all birds sharing an aviary share the same feeding-access condition. Thus aviary, not individual body-mass measurements or tracking fixes, is the independent unit of experimental exposure. There may be repeated identities/captivity effects; only original source records can settle nesting of treatment, aviary, year and individual.

## Source-only admission, before new exploratory effect fitting

1. Retrieve **only** the original public Figshare REST v2 article metadata `/articles/28660718`. Preserve original version, license, published date, exact file names, sizes, MD5 checksums as reported; do not claim MD5 is SHA256.
2. Read at most 15 original CSV, TSV, JSON, TXT, R-script or small ZIP files directly from **provider URL only**. **8 MiB per file** raw and uncompressed maximum; no access to closed user material, credentials, alternative mirrors or full GPS telemetry. Verify local MD5 when provided and compute SHA256 on actual downloaded bytes. No exact band/ring numbers, GPS coordinates, individual weather, release times or bird rows emitted.
3. Collect only **original field names, row counts and aggregate distinct values** for candidate `treatment`, `year`, `aviary`, `individual`, `release`, `migration departure`, `moult`, `body mass`, `mass gain rate`, `survival/detection` variables. Original R scripts may be inspected for input file names / model terms but not redistributed.
4. A *published treatment-to-departure* replication can be source-grain evaluated only when the original codebook identifies `treatment` 6/12/24h, unambiguous `aviary` and `year`, source individual identity, **actually observed** post-release departure or documented right censoring, and original year 2022–2023 inclusion. Reports of 97 birds in the experiment, 94/93 with physiological measurements, or "two aviaries per treatment per year" do **NOT** by themselves establish usable tracked departure observations.
5. **Aviary-cluster** or explicit original randomization unit must enter uncertainty; bird-level treating as independent replicates is forbidden. 2021 pilot cannot be assigned a fictitious observed migration departure. Do not join retrospective weather with GPS or infer breeding success.
6. All positive experiment results (more food→higher mass gain→earlier migratory departure) are **already author-published**. This experiment demonstrates the **existence of physiological flexibility/resource constraint** in one species, but does **not** manipulate predictive cue reliability, information disclosure times, Bayesian threshold `q_wait(D_eff)`, feasible spatial migration recourse `r(t)` at a matched decision, interactor asynchrony or fitness. No joint PAYOFF-B information × actionability result is identifiable.
7. If public original files only contain publication summaries, code without usable bird-aviary-year rows, or unauthorized data, record exact `SOURCE_DESIGN_NOT_ESTIMABLE` and stop. Never auto-fit an ecological coefficient because column names happen to match. No eBird or TinyFish.

## Current priority and scientific use

Compare this **manipulated feeding-time** paper as **prior art for resource-dependent physiological flexibility**, and Rüppel et al. (2023) as **prior art for environmental influences on sequential choice**. They are **distinct research systems and response grains**. Treating them as the two ends of a demonstrated **same-individual information-gated causal chain** would be scientifically false. Any future direct natural test requires the **same decision unit** to have independently measured precommitment cue availability, alternative available actions/energy, and post-decision tracking and payoff.
