# PAYOFF-B V4.5 final ecological takeaway — 2026-10-07

Status: **SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION FROZEN**

## One-sentence result

> **A seasonal environment can become more forecastable without giving a
> migrating organism correspondingly greater usable information, because
> forecastability, information access, retained actionability and correction are
> separate stages of tracking.**

## What the bird system establishes

The bird analysis directly establishes a change in the **external prediction
problem**:

- standardized source-target spring coupling increased;
- target spring variability increased;
- reconstructed regional nonlocal environmental structure gained strong
  out-of-sample analyst forecast value;
- the realized source mid-green-up event was not consistently online before the
  estimated migration front reached the mapped source stage;
- target green-up advanced while estimated population arrival changed little;
- larger route-level gains in analyst forecastability did not produce
  detectable bird-specific mismatch improvement beyond structural nulls.

Therefore the bird result is not:

> birds received better information but failed to use it.

It is:

> the environment became more forecastable to an analyst, but that
> forecastability cannot be equated with information available to the bird.

That is the empirical reason the access layer is required.

## What the bird system does not establish

The bird data do not directly identify:
- the cue perceived by individuals;
- organismal information value G_O;
- retained actionability r;
- individual feedback gain;
- the fitness optimum of arrival relative to mid-green-up.

The restricted source-target stage subset shows population-level phase
transformation, but measurement-error and subset-selection audits keep that
result supporting rather than mechanistic.

## What the mule-deer system establishes

The mule-deer source-data reanalysis supplies the missing individual-level
natural anchor for the downstream side:

- signed phase variance contracts strongly;
- early and late individuals change movement rate in opposite directions;
- stopover use changes in the opposite signed direction;
- most animal-years finish closer to peak green-up than they begin.

This correction phenomenon is prior work; the contribution here is to place it
on the same signed phase-state coordinate used by the framework.

## General ecological architecture

The final architecture is:

    environmental forecastability
        ->
    organismal information access
        ->
    retained actionability
        ->
    downstream correction
        ->
    realized seasonal timing.

Failure can occur at different interfaces.

A species may inhabit a highly forecastable environment yet lack access to the
relevant information at the decision stage. It may access the information but
have little remaining ability to alter timing. Or it may retain correction
options but fail to use them effectively.

These are biologically different failure modes even when the final observed
mismatch is identical.

## Why this is more than a predictability paper

The ecological result is not that correlation changed.

The stronger point is:

> **predictability is a property of an observer plus a prediction problem,
> whereas adaptation requires information to cross biological interfaces.**

This is why a correlation coefficient, a forecast-error metric and a realized
phenological mismatch should not be treated as interchangeable measures of
seasonal tracking.

## Journal-facing headline

Preferred:

> **Seasonal tracking depends on information access and opportunities for
> correction**

Do not headline:
- increasing spatial synchrony;
- increasing information;
- climate-driven information change;
- bird feedback correction.

## Higher-journal experiment implied by the paper

The decisive next test is one natural system measured repeatedly along an
ordered trajectory, with independent estimates of:

    external forecastability G_E
    + actual cue access / inference G_O
    + retained actionability r
    + behavioral response
    + phase outcome.

That experiment would turn the current architecture from an empirically
anchored synthesis into a direct mechanistic test.
