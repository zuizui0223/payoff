# PAYOFF-B V8 post-outcome publication decision — 2026-10-05

Status: **ROUTE B CLOSED; V8 RETAINED AS A FALSIFICATION / SUFFICIENCY STRESS TEST FOR V3**

## Bottom line

The preregistered V8 result does not support the proposed high-impact headline

> climate change is degrading the information migrants use to anticipate spring.

Under the frozen definition, the environmental result is decisively opposite:
source-destination green-up correlations became more positive.

The downstream analyses also do not support converting this opposite result
into a new causal headline. The correct use of V8 is therefore narrower and
more useful:

> **External environmental predictability did not deteriorate in this sampled
> system, yet increased predictive connectivity did not translate into the
> predicted improvement in bird mismatch.**

This is a falsification of a simple information-loss explanation and a stress
test of the idea that better environmental information is sufficient for
better realized timing. It does not identify the missing biological mechanism.

## Evidence ledger

### E1 — environmental V8: strong positive change

Frozen V8:
- 166 unique spatial source-target pairs;
- 28 species;
- mean delta-rho = +0.3690;
- 95% pair-bootstrap CI = +0.2984 to +0.4365;
- equal-species mean = +0.3364;
- 95% CI = +0.2628 to +0.4312;
- 26/28 species means positive.

Mandatory sensitivities retain the positive direction under:
- Fisher-z;
- exact complete windows;
- alternative 7-year windows;
- raw undetrended correlation;
- every leave-one-species-out analysis.

Therefore broad **degradation** is not merely unsupported; the sampled
environmental coordinate changed robustly in the opposite direction.

### E2 — bird mismatch did not show the registered transfer

Frozen transfer sample:
- 150 species-target rows;
- 72 unique spatial pairs;
- 22 species.

Registered transfer coefficient:
- beta = +0.06244;
- 95% pair-bootstrap CI = -0.01411 to +0.13635;
- registered direction = negative;
- status = NOT_SUPPORTED.

The earlier pooled 2026-09-26 coefficient (-0.04618) is more negative than the
lower bound of the transfer interval, so a temporal transfer effect at least as
negative as that empirical reference is excluded under the frozen benchmark
rule.

Across the transfer sample, mean late-minus-early log mismatch is near zero:
- unweighted = -0.00020, CI -0.07014 to +0.07195;
- equal-species = -0.02031, CI -0.09002 to +0.05123.

Thus no clear overall mismatch improvement accompanies the large environmental
connectivity increase.

### E3 — apparent positive transfer is structural, not a bird response

A fixed-arrival null gives:
- beta = +0.04742;
- observed minus fixed-arrival beta = +0.01502;
- 95% CI for the increment = -0.05212 to +0.10273.

Within-window arrival permutation:
- null median = +0.04677;
- null 95% interval = +0.01312 to +0.08188;
- observed beta = +0.06244.

Therefore the positive observed transfer slope can be generated by shared
target-green-up geometry and the mismatch transform. It must not be described
as increasing predictability worsening bird timing.

### E4 — stricter matched between/within decomposition is negative

On the same 150-row / 72-pair panel:
- between-route beta = +0.01218, CI -0.11108 to +0.08939;
- within-route beta = +0.05178, CI -0.01170 to +0.11308;
- between-within contrast = +0.03960, CI -0.04993 to +0.17550.

Thus the earlier broad pooled association does not reproduce as a supported
persistent negative between-route relationship in this stricter matched panel.

Do not build the V8 story around a static-information / dynamic-transfer
dissociation.

### E5 — V8 migration-speed actuator bridge is unavailable

Frozen repeated-speed admission:

- 4 unique pairs;
- 4 species.

Status:
`V8_ACTUATOR_BRIDGE = NOT_ESTIMABLE`.

No speed-change coefficient was calculated and thresholds were not relaxed.

## Relation to prior literature

The V8 environmental result is interesting but not, by itself, a top-journal
novelty claim.

- Kölzsch et al. (2015, Journal of Animal Ecology) established route-scale
  spring predictability and its relationship with migratory timing.
- Bauer et al. (2020, Philosophical Transactions B) explicitly called for
  repeated climatic-connectivity analyses over past decades to test whether
  climate change alters route correlations.
- Liu et al. (2019, Geophysical Research Letters) predicted increasing spatial
  synchrony of Northern Hemisphere spring vegetation phenology under warming,
  so stronger spatial coupling is biologically plausible rather than an
  isolated anomaly.
- Robertson et al. (2024, PNAS) showed across 150 species that migrations are
  generally more synchronized with climatological green-up than current-year
  green-up and that changing green-up influences mismatch.
- Schreven et al. (2026, Journal of Animal Ecology) directly showed temporal
  changes, including a sign change, in route-level spring predictability in
  Arctic geese.

The broad multi-species temporal test of the exact detrended
source-destination correlation remains distinctive, but its ecological
consequence is not demonstrated strongly enough here to carry an
Ecology Letters / PNAS / Nature Ecology & Evolution paper by itself.

## Publication decision

### Route B — Ecology Letters / PNAS / Nature Ecology & Evolution

**CLOSED on current evidence.**

The predeclared Route-B hinge required broad information degradation and a
downstream ecological consequence. V8 gives the opposite environmental sign,
and the predicted downstream transfer is not supported.

Do not rescue Route B by relabelling the positive environmental result as the
original success criterion.

### Route A — theory-led integrated paper

**RETAIN.**

Best current role for V8:
1. present the simple information-loss hypothesis;
2. show that it fails strongly under the frozen V8 test;
3. show that increased connectivity is not accompanied by the predicted
   mismatch improvement;
4. use this as motivation for why environmental information quality alone is
   an incomplete description of seasonal adaptation;
5. then use the independent timer-controller theory and mule-deer mechanism to
   describe the missing biological architecture.

V8 is therefore a **falsification layer**, not the mechanistic proof.

## Revised one-sentence paper logic

> Seasonal mismatch cannot be understood from environmental predictability
> alone: predictive connectivity can improve without a corresponding gain in
> realized tracking, because timing also depends on when organisms become able
> to act and how much downstream correction remains possible.

The first clause is directly supported by the V8 stress test.
The second clause is supplied by the independent theory and separate
individual-level evidence; V8 itself does not identify it.

## Manuscript architecture

Recommended main-text order:

**Fig. 1 — General theory**
Information can improve while actionability declines; timer and controller are
serial rather than interchangeable.

**Fig. 2 — Environmental falsification**
V8: source-destination predictive connectivity strengthened, not degraded.

**Fig. 3 — Sufficiency stress test**
The increased connectivity did not produce the preregistered improvement in
bird mismatch; show only the defensible transfer result and structural-null
audit, not the spurious positive slope as a biological effect.

**Fig. 4 — Individual mechanism**
Mule deer: nutritional state predicts entry timing, while signed phase predicts
downstream speed/stopover and phase variance contracts through migration.

The older pooled broad-bird result should be demoted to a supporting analysis
because its negative association does not reproduce in the stricter matched
V8 panel.
