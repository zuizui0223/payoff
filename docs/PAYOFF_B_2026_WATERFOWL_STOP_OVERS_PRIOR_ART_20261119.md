# PAYOFF-B — November 2026 waterfowl stopover prior-art audit

Date: **2026-11-19**. Status: **source-backed novelty and claim-boundary review, no new ecological effect fitted or source GPS opened**.

## Immediate scientific decision

A new peer-reviewed study now directly occupies the broad ecological claim “more stopovers facilitate phenological tracking,” and another 2026 paper by overlapping authors occupies “individual timing heterogeneity predicts variation in migratory green-wave tracking.” **These must not be claimed as fresh PAYOFF-B discoveries.**

The remaining candidate PAYOFF-B contribution is not another descriptive stopover count slope; it is the narrower mechanism:

> **Does a predictive environmental cue become observable before an irreversible seasonal decision, while movement/stopover correction is still possible—and can asynchronous information uptake cause interaction mismatch?**

This remains **prospective**, not newly measured in the cited 2026 waterfowl studies or the present PAYOFF data.

## 1. Original verified publications, dates and statistical units

### Liu et al. 2026, *Journal of Animal Ecology*

- Title: *More stopovers facilitate migratory waterfowl tracking shifting vegetation green-up.*
- Original article and original publication date: [DOI 10.1111/1365-2656.70395](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.70395), **5 November 2026**; independent [PubMed PMID 42989429](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42989429/).
- Study: **220 spring migration paths from five East Asian waterfowl species**, 2015–2023, tracked with high-resolution GPS and satellite-derived vegetation green-up.
- Original result: **a higher number of stopover sites is associated with stronger sensitivity/closer arrival timing alignment with breeding-ground green-up**, as described in the published abstract. This is a published observational association: stopover number was not randomized; the paper discusses potential updating of information but this is not a direct experimental assay of an actual cue-acquisition deadline.
- Raw tracking data: source authors say the GPS is in **Movebank and available on request**. Derived code/data are cited at Dryad [DOI 10.5061/dryad.pk0p2nh4p](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.pk0p2nh4p). **Neither original GPS rights nor the exact Dryad file schema was retrieved/verified in this audit**, so do not claim an independently re-fitted model.

### Liu et al. 2026, *Movement Ecology*

- Title: *Phenological tracking of migratory waterfowl to spring green-up derived from individual movement.*
- Original publication: [DOI 10.1186/s40462-026-00685-3](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40462-026-00685-3), **27 July 2026**; open full text [PubMed Central PMC13700227](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13700227/).
- Study: **323 spring tracks from 172 individuals, four waterfowl species** (greater white-fronted goose, lesser white-fronted goose, tundra bean goose, tundra swan), 2015–2022.
- Original result: the degree of long-run/interannual green-up tracking varies among species and with individual phenological variation. Great white-fronted geese showed relatively coherent tracking; tundra bean geese showed large individual variation and weaker tracking. There were **129 adjacent-year arrival-date pairs from 53 individuals** in the original study (for core stopover and breeding-area sites).
- **Sign of the effect of “more individual variation” is not universally beneficial**; the source explicitly contrasts intermediate with excessive variation.
- Same lead author/research lineage and overlapping years/taxa as the November paper. Original track ID overlap is **not audited**; do **not** count the two publications as independent new systems.

## 3. Interaction with PAYOFF's ongoing work

- The exact two-patch/two-season anti-phase migration optimum manuscript remains mathematically separate; the new 2026 papers do not replace or invalidate its conditional uniqueness result.
- PAYOFF-B's synthetic moving-landscape result (finite temporal bypass; spatial migration re-enters near the limit) remains a mechanistic constructive model, **not** the first natural discovery that stopovers help timing.
- The previously promoted E6 published Usui meta-analysis is already acknowledged as prior art and is further qualified by the source-policy eBird audit in [PR #404](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/pull/404) and its 13-study original within-study robustness in [PR #405](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/pull/405). These are not new independent field replications.
- The new independent Bylot nest–climate source audit [PR #406](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/pull/406) found a nominal post-2015 ceiling of **6** overlapping years (below a prespecified 10-year holdout gate), plus **no verified GPS-to-nest individual key**. It is a feasibility result, not fitness causation.
- **No frozen manuscript, numerical result, effect sign, model threshold or preregistration was retuned on seeing this recent literature.** Original source rights remain respected (raw Movebank GPS by request; no unauthorized data access, no eBird).

## 4. Publication recommendation

In any journal-facing PAYOFF-B introduction/discussion, state:

> *Recent individually tracked waterfowl studies show that greater stopover use and heterogeneous individual phenological responses are associated with spring green-up tracking. The unresolved question is whether information is usable at the decision deadline, and whether heterogeneous actionability among interacting organisms causes loss of synchrony even if potential environmental predictability remains high.*

That sentence **distinguishes prior art from a prospective PAYOFF-specific falsifiable prediction**. It should be reviewed as a future journal-facing scope note, **not silently inserted into the frozen abstract, data results or V7 prospective hypothesis**.

### Next data admission

A viable new empirical test requires **a source that independently observes (i) cue quality or observation timestamp before commitment, (ii) individual action and downstream correction opportunity, (iii) arrival/resource mismatch, and ideally (iv) fitness/interacting partner timing**, with original IDs and sufficient independent routes/years. Simply pulling Liu's raw paths and refitting stopover count without such components would not establish a new ecological mechanism.
