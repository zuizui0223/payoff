- Authenticate pinned source; require exact header, family counts, three phenology labels, food and temperature labels/codes. Normalize only documented Cold15 C / Cold 15 C spelling alias. Require each numeric and categorical treatment code to map bijectively to the corresponding semantic label; family numeric label also bijective. Source slots LarvaeN 1..6, planned key family × delay × room × food × slot. Audit absent planned keys, exact duplicated keys, duplicated Vial label and discrepancies without guessing identity. If all composite keys unique and exactly five planned slots absent, main strict analysis excludes both records with repeated Vial label. Sensitivity includes both if their composite keys differ and their full design fields agree internally. Otherwise stop on composite-key ambiguity; no deduplication chosen by outcome.
- Require 0/1 explicit survival, and positive finite duration/mass where recorded; dead rows must lack metamorphosis outcomes, check inconsistencies. Survival separately uses all observed flags, no missing=death. Duration and mass use surviving individuals with both observed endpoints; average larvae within family/cell then weight families equally. Use fixed intersection of families with complete support for all 12 cells for both strict and sensitivity independently, require at least 8. Report exclusions, actual family/cell support, no survivor-only effect presented as unconditional fitness.
- Within each room and food context, compare nominal 6/12-day treatment to 0: duration difference in days (negative suggests developmental acceleration), log mean mass ratio, survival risk difference. Nominal recovery ratio = negative duration contrast / assigned cold-treatment days; not actual hatch or calendar-date recovery, because actual individual hatch dates unavailable.
- For each room/delay, food restriction interaction = delayed-minus-normal difference under restricted minus ad libitum food, on duration and survival scales; mass difference of log ratios. For each food/delay, cold-room versus warm-room contrast of delay effects; also food × room × delay interaction; mark room-related effects conditional. Keep all four contexts and both delays regardless of signs.
- Use 10,000 whole-family bootstrap resamples of eligible families jointly across all 12 cells; no individual or separate cell resampling. Percentile intervals conditional on observed two rooms and families; not exact randomized inference or simultaneous intervals. Family bootstrap does not give replicated room uncertainty. Report leave-one-family-out point ranges for key duration interactions. Keep three endpoints separate; no causal mediation or survival-dependent interpretation of time. For survival additionally report intended-slot bounds by cell: lower all unobserved planned slots=0, upper all=1, equal family weights; bounds only, not imputation. With strict exclusions treat those records as unobserved for bounds.
