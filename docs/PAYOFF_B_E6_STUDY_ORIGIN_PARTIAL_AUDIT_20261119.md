# PAYOFF-B E6 — original primary-observation-source audit, 26/27 studies

Date: **2026-11-19**  
Status: **PARTIAL ORIGIN AUDIT ONLY**. `676/680` effect rows have published local or named original observation-system descriptions across twenty-six studies with **reported primary bird observation series distinct from eBird as the named source**. This does **not** certify that every secondary input, species grouping, effect estimate, or table is fully eBird-free. Unreviewed sources are not assigned an origin by exclusion.

Important classification nuance: the Ellwood 2010 study's input years end in **2007** and are explicitly historical naturalists' records; a **different 2025 follow-up** later combined those historical series with modern eBird data. This later follow-up does not retroactively establish that the 2010 study used eBird. Conversely, a named alternative primary data stream does not eliminate the need to examine each original study's full methods and any reused auxiliary input before certifying its derived E6 effect rows as fully eBird-independent.

For the 26 identified study samples combined: **676 effects = 99.41%** of the 680 rows after excluding the confirmed eBird study. Remaining **4 effects (0.59%) from one study, Zalakevicius 2002 (Lithuania)** still lack a paper-specific original-observation-source classification in this ledger. They remain **UNVERIFIED**, not silently labeled eBird-free or eBird-derived.

### Last remaining unsupported source identity

The original Usui source study **Zalakevicius (2002), *Biophysical impacts of climate change on bird populations and migration in Lithuania*** contributes **4 effect-size rows**. Its accessible original abstract says only that it presents data from Lithuanian studies of migration and climate, without uniquely identifying the study-specific arrival log, field station, original record custodian or observational sampling rules. Related Lithuanian ornithological stations such as Ventės Ragas or Žuvintas are **not automatically inferred** to be its source. It remains **ORIGINAL_OBSERVATION_SOURCE_UNVERIFIED**. <https://doi.org/10.1023/B:GEJO.0000003618.10088.ee>

For **Rubolini et al. 2007 (4 rows)** and **Ahola et al. 2004 (2 rows)**, the accessible primary abstracts establish a localized original phenology series and site, but do not uniquely identify the raw diary/database custodian. They are retained as **source-described local series only** and are not upgraded to raw-data-verified or globally eBird-free. The other new sources more specifically identify bird reports, monitoring schemes, reserve annals, first-sighting diaries or capture records. The provenance inventory is neither a claim of direct cue measurement nor of complete eBird exclusion in all ancillary weather/species data.

## What the executable inventory validates

`scripts/payoff_b_e6_source_origin_inventory.py` uses **only** the original checksum-verified GitHub archived E6 source-derived 944-row table. It matches each source study by an immutable bibliographic marker; validates expected count and exact one-study identity; excludes the known 264-row eBird study; guards against duplicate classifications; and requires a 676/4 split of the remaining 680 rows. It also reports distance-class counts for audit without fitting any new temperature-response coefficient, so effect signs or p-values **cannot influence source classification**. The companion tests and source-only GitHub Actions workflow ensure the same partition is reproduced in CI from the frozen artifact.

## Biological limits

- E6's **+0.608389 d/°C** adjusted migrant long-minus-short response in [PR #404](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/pull/404) remains a **forced published source exclusion of one known eBird-based study**, not a newly discovered effect or independent test.
- The 676 effect rows are **not** promoted as a newly fit "eBird-free subset" merely because their published original observational source is identifiable. Further original full-method and secondary-stream checking is necessary, and the remaining Lithuanian study (4 effects) is unverified.
- The dataset still lacks independent within-study crossing of temperature measurement site × migration-distance class, as shown in [PR #403](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/pull/403). It cannot identify migration decision-time cue access, recourse value, or causal biological information distance.
- Do not change frozen Paper 2 or V7, claim biological hysteresis, import fresh eBird observations, or merge draft PRs as part of this audit.
