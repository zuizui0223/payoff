## Interpretation and stop rule

The source-table/earlier secondary ledger covers 12 supplemented-only comparisons in three publications (including the O1-only TMC 2000 row; its O3 comparison remains quarantined). These all show a decline, but the availability-selected corpus is not a representative sample. For 11 paired comparisons, log supplement response change equals change in supplemented reproductive output minus change in natural output. This algebra is not causal mediation. Supplemented output is an operational treatment-specific performance measure, not an identified physiological maximum. Existing prior art already establishes temporal resource/pollen limitation and within-plant resource allocation. Do not market the pilot patterns as a novel general finding. Do not add provisional pairs to the main meta analysis. Prior threshold and primary theta count 0 remain unchanged. 

Next priority is targeted primary-source recovery of the five provisional papers, rather than enlarging the same outcome-screened corpus or adding more noncomparable taxa. If original uncertainty/unit cannot be authenticated, retain a descriptive study-level synthesis; no inverse-variance weights inferred from GloPL SD labels. A separate secondary synthesis question may be advanced after explicit source and independence admission, not a silent replacement of the primary hypothesis.
