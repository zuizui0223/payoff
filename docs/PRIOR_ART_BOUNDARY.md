# Prior-art boundary for PAYOFF

PAYOFF should not claim that specialization, modularity, frequency-dependent diversification, or evolutionary branching are new ideas. The literature already contains deep theory on all of them.

This note records the current boundary so the manuscript can focus on the actual bridge developed here.

## 1. Functional specialization and division of labor are established theory

Rueffler, Hermisson & Wagner (2012), **Evolution of functional specialization and division of labor**, PNAS 109:E326-E335, developed a general model in which modules contribute to two tasks under performance trade-offs and derived conditions favoring specialization/division of labor.

DOI: `10.1073/pnas.1110521109`

PAYOFF therefore does **not** claim to invent the idea that multifunctional modules can specialize when task trade-offs make generalism costly.

## 2. Pleiotropy and architecture can themselves evolve under functional trade-offs

Theory on the evolution of pleiotropy has explicitly asked when shared genetic components remain multifunctional versus specialize across functions.

PAYOFF therefore does **not** claim that the architecture connecting multiple functions is generally fixed or that the possibility of evolving integration is novel.

## 3. Frequency-dependent selection can alter genetic architecture

Work such as **The evolution of genetic architecture under frequency-dependent disruptive selection** (Evolution 60:1537-1550, 2006; DOI `10.1111/j.0014-3820.2006.tb00499.x`) already shows that frequency-dependent ecological selection can drive changes in genetic architecture.

PAYOFF therefore does **not** claim that frequency dependence and evolving architecture have never been linked.

## 4. Adaptive dynamics already provides invasion-fitness and branching frameworks

Geritz, Metz, Kisdi & Meszena (1997), **Dynamics of Adaptation and Evolutionary Branching**, formalized evolutionary dynamics in changing biotic environments with invasion fitness and branching into additional coexisting types.

DOI: `10.1103/PhysRevLett.78.2024`

A large adaptive-dynamics literature subsequently developed trade-off-driven branching and stable polymorphism.

PAYOFF therefore does **not** claim to invent reciprocal invasion analysis, coexistence under negative frequency dependence, or diversity generated by frequency-dependent selection.

## 5. Phenotypic integration and modularity are mature fields

Phenotypic integration/modularity theory already treats integration as both potentially constraining and potentially facilitating evolution, and modularization as permitting subsets of traits to vary more independently.

Recent synthesis continues to emphasize integration and modularity as central to diversification across biological scales.

PAYOFF therefore does **not** claim that reducing coupling among traits is a new concept.

The key question is not simply

> Can trade-offs cause specialization?

It is

> **Can an empirically reconstructed one-coordinate compromise be carried, on the same fitness scale, through a costed architecture transition and into reciprocal invasion predictions for the population dynamics of shared versus differentiated architectures?**

That is the SCH -> BALANCE -> BITA -> PAYOFF bridge.

## 7. Strongest current theorem-level pieces

### Exact quadratic receipt

For the declared residual-coupling model,

```text
R=sL
```

is exact rather than heuristic.

### Many-function modularity receipt

For the graph-Laplacian coupling model, increasing integration strength strictly reduces recovery when function-specific optima differ, and for `0<K<L_n` there is one critical integration strength.

### Boundary splitting

The static architecture crossing

```text
phi=0
```

splits under frequency dependence into two reciprocal invasion boundaries

```text
phi=eta
and
phi=-eta.
```

Their intervening region is stable coexistence for `eta<0` and mutual non-invasion / coordination bistability for `eta>0`.

### Minimal empirical identification

Two reciprocal rare-invasion contrasts identify both the frequency-independent architecture gap and linear frequency feedback:

```text
phi=(Delta0+Delta1)/2
eta=(Delta1-Delta0)/2.
```

This creates a direct empirical target that can receive quantities from the sister repositories rather than starting from an abstract strategy variable.

## 8. Claim ceiling

Until a broader systematic prior-art search is complete, manuscript language should use:

```text
we derive
we connect
we provide an explicit bridge
we show that under the declared model
```

rather than:

```text
first theory of specialization
first game theory of modularity
first demonstration that frequency dependence changes architecture.
```

The novelty claim should remain the **cross-scale estimand bridge and its testable reciprocal-invasion consequences**, not the existence of any component idea by itself.
