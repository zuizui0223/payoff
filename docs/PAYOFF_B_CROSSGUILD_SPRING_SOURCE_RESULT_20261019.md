Curlew source covers only the early portion of spring migration, and the mixed Anas package cannot be treated as a single taxon. These are real restrictions to acknowledge before any across-system coefficient.

The previous stork result with 53 dates/individual-years was based on a **winter-referenced** January–April proxy. The present 91 is the **within-February–May relative latitude-progress** proxy; the counts describe different scientific quantities, not conflicting reruns.

## Highest-value ecological question to test next

**When can environmental forecasts change migration decisions, and when does local information arrive too late for available correction?**

The existing preregistered PAYOFF V7R predictability × route recourse and V8 correlation-change → bird mismatch predictions were NOT SUPPORTED. Do not present the new tracking source readiness as confirmation of those rejected effects.

A new primary *causal or mechanistic* claim requires independent measurements of:

1. True individual migration stages and route geometry, including the presence of a destination/resident guild. The latitude 20%–80% checkpoints are screening proxies and are NOT departures, stopovers, or breeding arrivals.
2. Upstream environmental forecast information, trained only on historical climate/phenology prior to each outcome and demonstrably available to the focal bird before departure.
3. Locally available predecision cues measured independently of the bird's subsequent departure or speed, and route-stage-specific feasible correction opportunities estimated without reusing that same focal action.
4. Signed behavior, change in phenological phase error, and an independent vital rate only where actually recorded.
5. Species/study/flyway/year dependence, true cohort overlap and Argos/GPS accuracy. At least three **independent** focal migration cohorts are needed for a cross-taxon confirmatory claim; if such replication is unavailable, report descriptive sources only.

The next source admission should focus on verifying stagewise cue chronology and measurement—not broadening the species count by redefining season cutoffs after viewing outcomes. Do not fit the interaction coefficient until all admission requirements are frozen independently from the held-out behavior data.
