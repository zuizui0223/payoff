# PAYOFF-B V7 novelty audit — predictability × route-stage urgency

Date: **2026-10-05**

Status: **PREOUTCOME NOVELTY BOUNDARY**

## Question

V7 asks:

> **Does the influence of predictive environmental information on migratory
> timing depend on route-stage urgency / remaining opportunity?**

The intended test uses environmental predictability measured independently from
historical spring phenology and route position measured independently from bird
timing.

## Prior art that V7 does not own

### Bauer, Gienapp & Madsen 2008

Established that environmental cue relevance changes en route in pink-footed
geese.

Not novel:
- stage-dependent cue use;
- accumulated temperature affecting migration decisions;
- sequential environmental updating.

### Kölzsch et al. 2015

Established that:
- climatic predictability varies between consecutive stopovers;
- more predictable links are associated with migration timing closer to local
  spring;
- timing varies systematically along the route.

Not novel:
- predictive connectivity;
- predictability affecting timing;
- route position affecting timing.

### Bauer et al. 2020

Dynamic migration model established that:
- environmental predictability can improve timing and fitness;
- intermediate stopovers can be valuable information sources;
- information can be particularly valuable in the final migration step.

Not novel:
- information value varying among migration stages;
- final-step information value as a theoretical prediction.

### Theurich et al. 2026

Dark-bellied Brent goose GPS study established that:
- wind strongly affects spring departure probability;
- wind selectivity is strongest early in the departure window;
- cue selectivity weakens as seasonal urgency increases;
- baseline departure tendency increases as the deadline approaches.

Not novel:
- urgency-modulated cue use;
- declining willingness to wait for favorable weather near a seasonal deadline.

## Search result

Targeted searches were run for combinations of:
- environmental predictability × migration route stage;
- cue use × route position × urgency;
- predictive cue × distance to breeding;
- departure decisions × predictability × remaining distance;
- stopover information × deadline / urgency.

The search recovered the prior works above and general reviews of environmental
predictability and migration decisions.

It did **not** identify a natural study whose focal analysis jointly tests:
1. independently estimated predictive environmental information;
2. independently defined route-stage urgency / remaining opportunity; and
3. their interaction on migratory timing or cue-linked behavior.

This is a search boundary, not a proof of priority.

## Why the interaction is biologically useful

Current literature contains two opposing ecological expectations:

### Information-near-destination expectation

As migrants approach the breeding destination, nearby environmental conditions
may become more informative about the future target. Under this route,
predictive information should matter more late in migration.

### Urgency expectation

As the seasonal deadline approaches, migrants have less time to wait for ideal
conditions. Under this route, selectivity for otherwise useful information
should weaken late in migration.

The V7 interaction tests which tendency dominates in a natural migration
system.

## Candidate natural system

Kölzsch et al. 2015 barnacle geese provide:
- 40 complete spring migrations;
- three flyways;
- 16 generalized stopover regions;
- independently reconstructed 30-year phenology predictability;
- public Movebank tracking data;
- route geometry and breeding-site position;
- individual timing relative to local spring.

This system is chosen because q and route position can be defined without using
the focal timing response.

## Novelty claim allowed before results

Allowed:

> Prior work shows both that predictive environmental information improves
> migratory timing and that seasonal urgency can weaken cue selectivity. We test
> whether the timing benefit of predictive environmental information itself
> changes with route-stage urgency.

Not allowed:
- first demonstration that migrants use environmental information;
- first demonstration of stage-dependent cue use;
- first demonstration that urgency affects departure;
- first demonstration that predictive connectivity matters;
- any claim that route distance directly measures actionability.

## Fail-closed novelty rule

If a prior natural study is found that already tests the same independently
defined predictability × route-stage interaction on migration timing, V7 closes
before focal outcome analysis.
