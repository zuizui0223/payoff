# Wave 24: speed asymmetry experiment
Synthetic follow-up to Wave 23. No new biological data. Keep the same synchronous Wright-Fisher update and costs cA=cB=.12, qA=.9. Factorial qB=.6,.8 and synchronization gain k=.3,.5; these span a negative endpoint summed-payoff change, positive endpoint change with delayed invasion, and positive endpoint change with immediate invasion. No selection by observed simulation outcomes. N=1000,10000 per species, mutation mu=0,1e-5 symmetric per offspring, selection intensity of A fixed to 1 and B scaled by beta=.25,1,4 (not a change in raw payoff or generation time). Initial F frequencies .01; 4000 generations; 256 replicates per cell; two seeds 20261126 and 20361126. 48 cells per seed, 24576 trials. Compare the same factorial cells, including all outcomes. At every step record expected joint payoff, mismatch, frequency gap, fraction of time A>=.95 and B<.95, terminal frequencies and first joint95. Main metrics: mean cumulative signed payoff deficit relative to CC (2*(.5+k)) per generation, mismatch probability averaged over generations, B zero before A crosses its invasion threshold for delayed-invasion contexts, and joint95 ever. Also record deterministic frequency recursion. No pooling as biological evidence. Same fixed-N assumptions and arbitrary common payoff scale. Explicit distinction: intensity is sensitivity of log relative fitness to expected payoff, not measured evolution rate or generational duration. Negative signed deficit means benefit; do not call it loss. All comparisons descriptive, no arbitrary significance filtering. No access to Pardikes raw data or alteration of frozen primary estimand.
