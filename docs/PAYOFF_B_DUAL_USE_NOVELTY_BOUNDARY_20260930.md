# PAYOFF-B dual-use information novelty boundary

Date: **2026-09-30**  
Status: **literature-screened claim boundary; not an exhaustive proof of priority**

## Bottom line

The generic value of information is established decision theory. Sequential
information, multiple uncertainty sources, fitness value of information,
adaptive-management VOI, stopover information and behavioral compensation are
all prior art.

The candidate PAYOFF-B contribution is narrower:

> A cue that is worth waiting for because it informs a seasonal action can also
> reduce the downstream cost created by that waiting. Under a declared
> separable binary model, this makes the effective deadline cost cue-dependent
> and yields exact information-use thresholds, rescue intervals and pairwise
> asynchronous-uptake geometry.

No located source in this screen states that exact ecological construction or
its closed-form deadline consequences. This is a **screen result**, not a claim
that all mathematical or ecological literature has been exhaustively searched.

## Why the generic VOI literature does not by itself establish the PAYOFF-B result

General sequential decision theory already allows information to alter multiple
future decisions. PAYOFF-B should not claim otherwise.

The narrower construction adds three ecological restrictions simultaneously:

1. the downstream compensation problem exists **because the actor waited**;
2. the focal cue is acquired only by waiting past a decision deadline;
3. actors interact, so different resulting information-use thresholds can
   create ecological mismatch and coordination traps.

The first two generate the dual-use deadline geometry. The third connects that
geometry to the broader PAYOFF-B coordination theory.

## Why migration literature does not by itself establish the result

Existing migration work separately supports the biological pieces:

- stopovers can provide information about later conditions;
- environmental predictability changes optimal migration timing;
- animals can compensate en route by changing speed and stopover use.

Those findings establish plausibility of information acquisition and
compensation, but do not by themselves derive a cue-dependent waiting cost
(D_{eff}(q)), an exact reliability threshold, or a pairwise asynchronous
information-use window.

## Safe novelty language

Use:

> We derive an exact information-deadline model in which the same environmental
> cue can inform both a seasonal action and a downstream compensation decision.
> Under a declared separable binary model, compensation information makes the
> effective cost of waiting cue-dependent and yields closed-form rescue and
> desynchronization conditions.

Avoid:

> We introduce the idea that information can affect multiple decisions.

Avoid:

> We introduce value of information to ecology or migration.

Avoid:

> Information values from different uncertainties are generally additive.
