Primary secondary contrast of duration in days: I = (mean duration delayed − normal in same mixed containers) − (mean duration delayed in homogeneous tanks − mean duration normal in homogeneous tanks). Positive I means stronger delay-associated developmental duration in mixed groups, not necessarily greater calendar mismatch. Separate descriptive log-mean interaction for mass, same arm groups: log(M_delay,mixed/M_normal,mixed)−log(M_delay,single/M_normal,single). ## Prior art and next boundary
Priority competition is already shown by the source paper, and Orizaola et al. 2020 Oikos 10.1111/oik.07919 experiments cross temperature, food and cold-induced delay. A second independent source is required before interpreting broad cross-system patterns. Hegyi et al. 2013 Oecologia 10.1007/s00442-012-2487-5 combines 23 years of food-peak and breeding records with a 3-year experimental short deprivation, but timing is observational and 24 of 33 experimental trios retained; source outcome/family covariance not yet verified. Do not claim novelty of competition limiting plasticity, immune trade-offs, or diet substitution alone.
