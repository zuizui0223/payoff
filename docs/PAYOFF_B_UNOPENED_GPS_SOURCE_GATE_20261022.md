The same original algorithm used in the previously opened sources is
applied **without change**: timestamp, unique animal/deployment
identifier, source WGS84 position; at least two original UTC hourly bins
per day in February–May; 5 consecutive days, first 3 stationary within
30 km, next day >=30km away, following day >=120km and >=0.8°
northward; 7-day separation between source candidates.

Report per-taxon aggregate GPS year/season sampling counts and all source
gate exclusions. Source eligibility requires at least eight original
animal-years with spring GPS source days, at least ten fixed
stationary→northward source events, and at least ten original animal-years
with those observed events. These are scientific **feasibility
thresholds**, not fitted signal/exposure/fitness effects. A legitimate
zero-event result is preserved and never turned into a false
'no behavioral response' inference.

This source audit does NOT establish actual stopover exit, bird
perception of weather, information deadline, response to a signalled
forecast, independent feasible recourse, phenological matching or
Darwinian fitness. Never upload individual GPS, animals' identities or
nest/endangered species locations. The vulture dataset contains
endangered species location data; derived output is aggregate-only.

The earlier published findings on goose foraging/fuel and vulture
habitat selection are **prior art**. Do not rebrand them PAYOFF-B
discoveries. Independent cohort identity must be checked before a
new prospective hypothesis test.
