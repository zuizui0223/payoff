# PAYOFF-B clock-portfolio natural-test stop rule

Date: **2026-10-03**  
Status: **post-freeze fail-closed rule; frozen GEB V2 unchanged**

## Decision

The current direct-test screen is closed with

NO_DIRECT_NATURAL_PORTFOLIO_FRAGILITY_TEST_ADMITTED.

No existing PAYOFF-B system jointly supplies:
- signed entry phase before perturbation;
- independently estimated historical feedback reliance;
- independently defined effective opportunity loss;
- post-perturbation final phase on the same coordinate;
- adequate control of selection/observation reweighting.

Therefore the clock-portfolio fragility theorem remains a prospective mechanistic prediction.

## No same-data rescue

Do **not** reopen or re-label the following anchors as direct confirmation by changing endpoints, thresholds or definitions after seeing their results:

- industrial mule deer;
- piping plover / Hurricane Dorian;
- pink-footed goose / Filsø;
- great knot / Yellow Sea;
- current curlew / goose / godwit route datasets.

These systems can continue to anchor separate ingredients only.

## What would reopen the empirical lane

A dataset can enter the direct-test lane only if, before the focal downstream outcome is analysed, it passes all admission requirements in:

data/payoff_b_clock_portfolio_direct_test_screen_20261003.json

A genuinely new variable or independently released linked dataset can reopen a previously screened biological system, but the new information must solve the specific missing identification requirement rather than merely increase sample size.

Examples:
- destination-arrival timing newly linked to the same pre/post piping-plover individuals;
- independently reconstructed historical feedback reliance for a disturbed route;
- an externally quantified effective opportunity-retention measure after rerouting/substitution;
- repeated-individual final phase with explicit survival/observation tracking.

## Claim rule

Until a system passes admission, manuscript language is restricted to:

- exact theory;
- opportunity-loss anchors;
- opportunity-substitution anchors;
- selection/reweighting anchors;
- prospective predictions.

Do not write:
- "validated portfolio fragility";
- "confirmed hidden clock portfolios in nature";
- natural estimates of s_feedback, omega, or the fragility factor unless independently identified by the admitted design.

## Why this matters

The theorem predicts a within-architecture response to lost correction opportunity.

Population-level change after habitat disturbance can instead arise from substitution or selection/reweighting.

Closing the lane now protects that mechanistic distinction.
