# PAYOFF-B: same-individual mechanism-identification boundary across public data

Date: 2026-10-08
Status: **source-backed cross-study design audit; no new causal result**.
Scope: identify which *actually measured constructs* can test the causal
Bayesian information + recourse + energetic fitness + ecological interaction
claim. This note does NOT join different cohorts as one natural experiment.

## One causal process and six noninterchangeable layers

1. Distant future biological target: actual destination phenology and, if
   claiming selection, an independently estimated fitness-optimal target.
2. Available decision-time cue: a dated quantity potentially observed at
   the departure decision (not an after-the-fact realized destination date).
3. Actor's state or belief: ideally a cue response under an independent
   forecast/calendar competitor; repeatability alone is not private belief.
4. Actual action: departure decision, route/speed/stopover change, with
   independently bounded physical/physiological alternatives (recourse).
5. Ecological cost: nutritional/energetic resource state before and after
   the action, with confounding addressed.
6. Individual fitness and heterospecific interaction: reproductive/survival
   consequences and paired life-history partners, rather than a single
   remotely sensed green-up proximity score.

An observed association between ANY two layers cannot identify all six.

## What the three currently examined sources actually contain

| Source/cohort | Environment and cue timing | Actual decisions | Effort and later fitness | Identification result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amaral broad migratory birds (2002–2017; V8) | Range-cell environmental greenup across periods, spatial correlation; actual sensory cue / individual forecast unobserved | Species-cell mean modeled arrival; neither individual repeated decisions nor verified route-actuator set | No individual energetic or demographic outcomes | **V8 primary environmental correlation gained +0.369, but registered transfer to timing NOT SUPPORTED. No mechanistic cue or recourse identification** |
| Schindler 2024 Greenland white-fronted geese (2018–2022) | Day/stage, locally summarized weather, feeding, activity; no route-specific destination future greenup forecast or recorded information perception | Verified 642 spring rows = 107 bird-years, 49 individuals, all 6 stages; Iceland staging start and exit, start early breeding | Source fitness categories 28 success / 72 failure / 7 deferral; original authors already modeled arrival, energy and success | **Calendar-like staging exit with repeatable individual offsets; no identified active correction or staging-duration-specific added held-year fitness forecast** |
| Burnside 2021 Asian houbara (2014–2020 described spring cue test) | **Published** eight-day temperature prior to departure and eight-day temperature after arrival, GPS locations; raw Zenodo original XLSX metadata gate initiated | **Published** repeated individual departure and arrival of 48 GPS-tracked birds; original paper already established individually repeatable temperature threshold | No energetic cost or individual reproductive payoff promised by public Zenodo description; no verified within-route checkpoint recourse yet | **Good candidate for independently timed origin cue; source/schema and presence of stagewise recourse not yet admitted. Novelty limited by original publication** |

Do not treat temperature *extracted around* departure as proven cognitively used just because the original paper found a consistent statistical threshold; alternative individual site/route effects were directly tested by Burnside's null and require respecting its actual result.

**No permitted cross-dataset causal synthesis:** houbara and goose observations involve distinct taxa, regions, periods and individual identities; V8 gridded migration records are not the same individual migrants. Never match an Amaral environment panel to a Schindler reproductive outcome as if it were a biological individual track. Analogies can inform theory but are not linked evidence for a single mediation/fitness model.

## The strongest valid next experiment

A time-stamped within-individual comparative design, ideally with **exogenous variation** in environmental cue access or route recourse, would compare:

- An annual-calendar plus persistent individual effect null (already supported as a plausible goose competitor).
- A local-temperature/photoperiod rule (already prior-art supported in houbara).
- A sequence of updated cue beliefs along the route with explicitly observed post-cue speed/stopover choices.
- A resource- and energy-aware control policy whose predictions concern actual survival/reproduction, not merely greenness alignment.

To differentiate the last two, predeclare a decision checkpoint when the cue
became observable *and when unexercised adjustment capacity remained*. Compare
behavior before and after newly available information while holding calendar,
individual history and common weather as competing explanations.
A simultaneous energy/reproduction endpoint must be independently measured
and a biologically relevant seasonal target independently specified.

## Stop rules

- If houbara Zenodo file lacks stagewise decisions, classify as
  **PREDEPARTURE_CUE_REPLICATION**, not active checkpoint-control discovery.
- If only a predicted spring target and realized timing exist, classify as
  **TIMING_FORECAST**, not selection or reproductive fitness.
- If the available fitness study has stages but no independent cue/new
  information, classify as **CALENDAR_VERSUS_FEEDBACK_ALIAS**.
- If actual management experiments or natural instruments are unavailable,
  no causal conclusion about waiting/cost/recourse is permitted.
- Do not add more partial Gaussian toys or infer fitness weights from observed
  calendar offsets.
- The preregistered V7R, V8 negative findings remain negative, including
  the unsupported Q×population-recourse and correlation-change→mismatch
  effects. Post-result explorations do not retroactively justify them.

## Publication boundary

The integrated PAYOFF-B Paper 2 can be defended as a **theory-led framework
with competing natural evidence, prior-art guardrails and falsified simple
mechanisms**. It cannot claim a newly verified universal "hidden spring
Bayesian game" in birds, plants and pollinators, nor that environmental
information failed despite being used, nor a demonstrated individual
fitness-optimal undertracking mechanism. Those are the *next questions*.
