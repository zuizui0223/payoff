# PAYOFF-B independent taxon source test: vulture does not replicate stork solar association

2026-10-22. **Predeclared direction on held-out source weather; exploratory observational source-pattern test, not evolutionary mechanism confirmation.**

Original all-446 Movebank metadata source screening selected Neophron percnopterus (Egyptian vulture) as a CC0 independent-taxon candidate. GPS DOI 10.5441/001/1.385gk270, original full GPS MD5 578c45678d402d055000ea14e754b6da. The source itself contains an endangered bird, so no original routes or individual locations are distributed.

Before this taxon's weather data were opened, the following hypothesis and method were locked: the **negative case-minus-stay solar shortwave anomaly** observed in historical Ciconia ciconia physical environment would recur in a second bird often using soaring flight, when both are compared inside original 0.5-degree grid×calendar-year×month cells and physical weather is from source day−1 vs the previous seven days. This was a *post-stork, prospectively checked independent-source descriptive hypothesis*, not an original preregistered PAYOFF-B core hypothesis.

## Original source data and complete public environmental coverage

New vulture original GPS: 577,329 valid fixes, 16 unique bird IDs, 29 original February–May animal-years; same existing locked 3-day stationary→2-day northward algorithm produced 30 seven-day-deduplicated candidate movement events in 18 animal-years. The complete observational source at-risk day universe contains **1,859** windows: **33** raw future northward-labelled, **1,454** continued-stay, **232** other movement and **140** tracking-censored source windows.

**192/192** NASA POWER UTC daily environmental batch queries succeeded, covering all **1,859/1,859** source opportunities. Among 18 matched 0.5-degree grid/year/month strata there are just **19** original northward case-days. All source time/weather chronology gates were held fixed before opening the vulture solar results.

## Predeclared solar result and failure to generalize

| Distinct published taxon | Case-minus-stay solar anomaly (W/m²), within matched 0.5° grid×year×month | Descriptive 95% source weather-block interval |
|---|---:|---|
| Ciconia ciconia (previously opened) | **−17.10** | [-25.13, -7.47] (broad year-month) |
| Neophron percnopterus (newly opened) | **+2.91** | [-15.27, +23.52] (fine grid-month) |

The primary source-pattern transfer prediction required a **negative vulture solar contrast**; its sign was instead positive and the confidence/sensitivity interval crossed zero. **The stork solar association was NOT replicated as a robust shared physical pattern in this independent taxon.**

In the vulture raw pooled source observations the difference had been −6.92 W/m², but after the fixed same-place/year/month comparison it became +2.91 W/m². This highlights sensitivity to observational environment, not evidence that one soaring species favors clouds while another avoids them. The taxa differ in season, habitat, region, sensor histories, physical weather and stage, and there is insufficient power to establish an actual between-taxon biological difference.

Secondary post-outcome observation: vulture physical temperature anomaly difference was −1.01°C, with [-1.50, −0.41] under broad year-month weather-block resampling. It is **not** part of this source's fixed primary replication hypothesis and cannot be treated as newly confirmed cue-use ecology. Only 11 year-month blocks (effective case-weight n≈9.26) contribute. Within-**animal** same-grid/month estimator failed its >=10 animal-identity gate (only eight).

## Biological conclusion ceiling

No true stage-specific departure instant, actual perceived cue, available future weather forecast, independent feasible stopover response, destination mismatch, breeding or survival benefit was verified. Solar shortwave is not a measured convective updraft. Wind components at 10m are not route-projected flight-height tailwinds. The relevant original published vulture conservation ecology is prior art.

The earlier preregistered V7R/V8 and stork 53-season timing non-support remain untouched. This does not prove or disprove a universal information×deadline effect; it **rejects promotion of the previously observed solar source pattern into a cross-taxon generalization**.
