## Design gate before opening a new effect
Verify exact checksums and read original Variables/Info sheet; inspect unique larvae keys (globally and within family), family-to-parent mapping, parent IDs potentially reused, phenotype code/label and immune treatment code/label cross-tabs; report all 2×3 cells and complete-family support. Do not use mass/response values to classify treatments. Check endpoint-specific missingness without exporting biological values. Family clusters may share male/female parents; compute connected components of families sharing recorded same-sex parent identifiers, and numbers/sizes. Do not interpret distinct IDs as proof of independent experimental units, family biological independence, or separate cohorts from the JAE study. No new outcome effect in this audit. For a later family-balanced secondary fit, independently authenticate developmental-duration and mass units, the assignment/rearing unit and exact code semantics; use a separate contrast PHA vs APBS for handling/sham, APBS vs untreated control for handling effects, both phenology levels. Require positive mass/duration, preserve valid signed immune response, and no missing=zero. If family units or parental dependence cannot support interval estimation, report hold. No primary theta.
