# PAYOFF-B Scotland: caterpillar number-versus-mass phenology prefit gate
2026-11-16. This is a source/measurement audit after the exploratory **negative** Scottish prey-count/fledging LOYO+LOSO result in PR #389. It is not an independent confirmatory test and will fit **no** new fitness coefficient.

Official EIDC OGL DOI 10.5285/b63d53ae-2fe7-4ce9-82a2-2325098489e3. Original branch-beating caterpillars (counts per marked branch, 30 standard hits) and source caterpillar.mass (g per sample, measured from 2017). Source explicitly uses less-than 0.02 g for below-detection values, so it is interval censored, *not* 0. Flagged unreliable mass. Empty mass with strictly positive caterpillar count is MISSING. Empty mass with zero caterpillars (from 2017) is a structural zero under the mass-sum definition. Records before 2017 are NOT used for mass even when count zero. Source rows with uncertain mass, negative or unexplained mass fail closed. Convert grams to milligrams by 1000 and retain source units metadata.

For each original site/year/day, require at least 90% branch samples have admissible mass or zero-count structural zeros, including censored intervals. Aggregate separately into sample-effort-average lower and upper mg per standardized beating, not standing biomass per m2. Count metric remains per beating. No use of fitted phenological Gaussian, no interpolation outside measured support, no gap >8 days.

For all source-verified unmanipulated blue-tit nests with observed hatch/fledged/clutch, calculate 14-day hatch-based potential mass exposure interval from admitted lower and upper observed daily mass curves, minimum 14/14 days supported. Report eligible nests, site-years and years, mass exposure [low,up], separately from count exposure. **Do not fit or optimize an outcome.** Source mass missing for 2014–16, and earlier count/fitness admission yields at most four 2018–2021 years; therefore the same >=5-year LOYO GO criterion cannot be satisfied. If an actual independent fifth year emerges from the strict original source, audit its treatment/hatching definitions before considering fit.

Independent descriptive measurement contrast: on site-years with both valid source full-season mass and count series, report first observed date of each seasonal peak, difference mass minus count (days), median absolute difference, proportion with absolute difference >=7 and >=14 days, and mass peak bounds when censor choices differ. Count site-year only once; no sample-level p-value. This seasonal number/biomass difference is subject to known prior art Macphie et al. 2025 Oikos and Versluijs et al. 2024, so no novel biological mechanism is claimed.
