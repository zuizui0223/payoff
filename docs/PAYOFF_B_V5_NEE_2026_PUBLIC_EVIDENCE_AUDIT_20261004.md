# PAYOFF-B V5 — public-evidence audit of Brlík et al. 2026 collision

Date: **2026-10-04**  
Status: **HOLD CONFIRMED AT CURRENT PUBLIC-EVIDENCE LIMIT**

## Purpose

This audit follows
`docs/PAYOFF_B_V5_NEE_2026_COLLISION_HOLD_20261004.md`.

The task is not to prove priority from title similarity. It is to determine
whether currently public information is sufficient to:

1. reopen V5 because the Nature Ecology & Evolution paper is biologically
   distinct; or
2. stop V5 because the accepted paper already occupies the remaining
   cross-transition annual-cycle linkage claim.

No V5 H1/H2 model or new effect extraction was opened during this audit.

## Confirmed public facts

The accepted/in-press study is:

Brlík V, Procházka P, Rushing CS, Storch D, Schmaljohann H, et al.,
Norris DR. 2026. **Temporal links in avian migration schedules across the
annual cycle.** *Nature Ecology & Evolution*, in press.

Independent public author/institution pages confirm the title, journal and a
very large international tracking consortium.

Vojtěch Brlík's ORCID also lists the associated public dataset:

- master DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18175801;
- version records: 10.5281/zenodo.18175802,
  10.5281/zenodo.18433481 and 10.5281/zenodo.19821864;

A public institutional repository search result exposes the beginning of the
study framing:

> Each year, migratory birds move thousands of kilometers across

> annual-cycle events are linked and how much flexibility exists in migration

The quote is intentionally truncated at the point exposed publicly. No missing
text is reconstructed.

## Why the collision is substantive

The overlap is now supported by both title and problem framing.

V5 asks:

> Where in the annual cycle do migratory birds absorb temporal delays, and
> what predicts when those delays persist?

Brlík et al. publicly frame their accepted study around:

- links among annual-cycle events;
- flexibility of migration schedules;
- vulnerability to environmental change.

These are the same biological objects that motivated the remaining V5
cross-transition claim.

The overlap is strengthened by the consortium composition. Public author lists
include many investigators and tracking programmes that are also represented in
the same full-annual-cycle source literature from which V5 is drawing effects.

This does not prove identical estimands, but it makes a cosmetic distinction
unacceptable.

## What remains unavailable

The following could not be safely obtained from current public indexing:

1. the complete accepted abstract;
2. the accepted manuscript;
3. the final Nature DOI;
4. the full Methods;
5. the full Results;
6. the Zenodo file inventory or file bytes;

Therefore the audit still cannot establish whether Brlík et al.:

- estimate unstandardized day/day propagation;
- compare stationary versus active migration transitions;
- test available interval duration;
- quantify transition-specific links across more annual-cycle classes than V5;
- include direct fitness/vital-rate outcomes;
- use a dataset sufficiently distinct from all current V5 cohorts.

### B — distinct biological transition contrast

### C — distinct fitness / conservation consequence

**NOT ESTABLISHED.**

vital rates or disturbance response is not yet known.

## Effect-extraction consequence

No additional timing-propagation effect should be promoted while this hold is
active.

This includes newly recoverable candidates such as Tawny Pipit. Such values may
be kept in private scratch work or source notes, but adding them to the primary
effect file would increase sunk-cost pressure without resolving publication
novelty.

The nine already opened source-faithful effects remain unchanged provenance.

## What would change this decision

The hold can be resolved only by new publication information, not by further
V5 effect accumulation.

Valid triggers are:

1. the Brlík et al. accepted manuscript or version of record becomes publicly
   inspectable;
2. the associated Zenodo package exposes methods/code sufficient to identify
   the focal estimands and transition classes;
3. an author/institutional repository exposes the complete abstract or methods
   at a level that resolves one of reopening routes A–C.

Until then, the scientifically correct action is to stop corpus expansion.

## Interpretation

The collision is useful evidence about field position.

PAYOFF-B converged from an abstract information/control theory through
full-annual-cycle fitness and finally to a concrete ecological question that a
large migration-ecology consortium independently identified as important.

That supports the **importance of the biological question**.

It does not support independent **publication novelty**.

Those two judgments must remain separate.
