# PAYOFF-B Aikens routing amendment for information-deadlines v2

Date: **2026-09-27**  
Status: **OUTCOME-BLIND ROUTING FROZEN BEFORE AIKENS LAMBDA OPENING**

## Decision

The preregistered Aikens industrial-development phase-retention outcome is no
longer part of the main-text argument of
`PAYOFF_B_INFORMATION_DEADLINES_V2_PREOUTCOME.md`.

This routing decision is made **before opening the registered Aikens lambda
outcome**.

Regardless of whether that outcome is supported, null, opposite-signed within
uncertainty, or NOT_ESTIMABLE, it will be reported as a
**Supplementary actuation/capacity boundary test**.

It may not change:

- the v2 title;
- the 150-word abstract;
- the information-timing mechanism;
- the non-monotonic desynchronization result;
- the topology-dependent memory result;
- the broad predictive-connectivity interpretation;
- the flycatcher decision-time anchor;
- the wigeon null interpretation;
- the long-term cue-driver negative gate;
- journal routing.

## Why the routing changed

The old integrated v1 manuscript was organized around movement, timing,
actuation and phase retention. In that architecture, a within-taxon actuation
perturbation was a central discriminating test.

The v2 manuscript asks a different, narrower question:

> what happens when useful information becomes available after interacting
> species face different decision deadlines?

Aikens remains scientifically useful for separating response capacity from
information constraints, but it no longer discriminates among the central v2
mechanisms.

Keeping an unopened result in the main narrative would therefore add story
count rather than test the new headline.

## Supplementary role

The Supplementary Information should retain:

1. the frozen industrial-development actuation result;
2. the preregistered Aikens lambda contract;
3. the rendered lambda result once opened;
4. a short statement explaining whether actuation attenuation propagates into
   phase retention under the registered support gate.

The result is reported for completeness and programme integrity, not as a
criterion for the v2 claim.

## Outcome-blind wording rule

Permitted main-text sentence after the result is frozen, if needed:

> A separately preregistered within-taxon actuation perturbation is reported in
> Supplementary Information; its outcome does not alter the information-timing
> results.

No result-specific adjective or direction belongs in the main text unless a
reviewer explicitly requires it.

## Programme consequence

PAYOFF-B therefore remains a two-paper programme.

Aikens is **not** promoted into a third overlapping paper by this amendment. It
is retained as registered supplementary evidence associated with Paper 2 and as
programme provenance.
