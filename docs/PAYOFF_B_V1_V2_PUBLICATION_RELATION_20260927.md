## What V2 absorbs from V1

- local movement/timing substitutability;
- finite temporal buffering;
- spatial re-entry under sustained forcing;
- fragmentation exposure;
- phase-retention / actuator decomposition;
- the registered broad-bird and direct migration evidence;
- the unopened Aikens within-taxon perturbation gate.

These results now support the broader information-coordination story rather
than define the main headline.

## What changes in the headline

V1 headline:

> Temporal buffering delays but does not permanently replace spatial tracking.

V2 headline:

> Information use is itself a coordination state: interacting organisms can
> begin using the same improving information at different decision thresholds,
> and after coordinated information use collapses, even perfect environmental
> information need not restore ecological coordination.

The V1 conclusion remains valid as a capacity result. It is not the primary
Paper 2 novelty claim.
