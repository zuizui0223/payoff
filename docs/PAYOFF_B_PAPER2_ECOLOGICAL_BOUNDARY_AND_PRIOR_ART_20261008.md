# PAYOFF-B Paper 2 ecological claim-boundary audit

Date: 2026-10-08. Scope: manuscript interpretation and prior art ONLY.
Base: draft PR #306, V8-integrated V4 manuscript.
Frozen V3, V7R and V8 source, outcomes, figures, checksums and inferential decisions **unchanged**.

## Five distinct ecological estimands

| Layer | Definition | Directly established by existing PAYOFF-B? |
|---|---|---|
| Spatial environmental connectivity | Signed, detrended interannual green-up correlation between geographical range-derived source and destination | YES (V8; 166 unique spatial pairs, 28 species; mean delta rho +0.369) |
| Decision-time calibrated forecast | Heldout prediction of a biologically relevant future state using only cues the animal could sense at decision time | NO; historical correlations are not belief/sensing or prospective forecast calibration |
| Individual actionability | Individual physically and physiologically feasible route/stopover/timing choices after cue availability | NO for macro birds; empirical population-time envelopes are only proxies; V8 speed bridge NOT ESTIMABLE |
| Phenological matching | Bird arrival-green-up timing error (range/target proxy) | YES with caveats; registered V8 correlation-gain -> improvement transfer NOT SUPPORTED |
| Evolutionary / demographic fitness efficacy | Survival or reproductive success under cue, timing, and energy/arrival tradeoffs | NO in V8; route matching and time gains are not fitness |

**Key response**: V8 did NOT show 'information quality increased but birds failed to use it'. It showed *environmental correlations increased* while a particular prespecified matching-transfer prediction was not supported. No causal attribution among obsolete information, fixed calendar, limited recourse or rational costly undertracking is licensed.

## Strongest natural same-population evidence and its limit

Ortega mule deer: out of 93 source-joinable IFBFat-migration years, 62 animal-years / 40 animals have March condition temporally before April-or-later migration. March IFBFat is associated with earlier starts (standardized -3.97 d/unit, 95% animal-cluster CI -6.31 to -0.69; year-FE uncertainty spans zero). Signed incoming phase predicts later speed and stopover in the same subset while direct IFBFat coefficients for those actuators are unresolved. But the prospective phase-by-IFBFat *gating* interactions were unsupported:
- movement rate beta -0.01517; CI -0.05425 to +0.02190;
- stopover beta +0.04812; CI -0.15392 to +0.23648.
These findings support a descriptive entry-state vs downstream-response separation candidate, NOT direct physiological gating of correction. The source paper had already documented different actuator response and absence of nutritional condition influence on speed/stopover.

## Nonoptional prior-art constraints

- Torstenson and Shaw (2025), *Oikos* 2025:e10862, first online 2024-10-18; DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/oik.10862, already modelled how cue type (environmental versus temporal) can reverse relative fitness performance depending on seasonality. Explicitly distinguished cue accuracy and efficacy. PAYOFF-B cannot introduce either distinction or broad cue reversal as a first.
- Jonzén et al. (2007), *Proceedings B*, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1685845/; rational early arrival and movement costs can make incomplete phenology matching optimal with correct information.
- Abrahms et al. (2021), *Nature Communications* https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-27626-5; whooping cranes already exhibit life-stage social and experiential learning of migration timing, stronger adult resource response, and lower snow exposure with age. No standalone novelty for age/experience effects.
- Kölzsch et al. (2015), *Journal of Animal Ecology*, https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.12281; historical route-scale spring predictability and timing association already established, including limitations of partly fixed schedules.
- PAYOFF V7R PR #310: registered Q×R not supported (exact within-flyway p=0.56994), negative stopover covariances not identified active feedback; PR #313 shows fixed calendar counterexample.

## What the current V4 contribution can honestly be

A *theory-led synthesis* separating entry readiness and route-stage timing control, tested against independent natural directional examples and a prospective environmental-correlation falsification. Its distinctiveness is the **specified serial architecture and its boundaries**, not a newly confirmed universal biological mechanism and not a novel generic signal-processing theorem.

The paper can state that an environmental-correlation-only explanation is inadequate for describing the observed sources of seasonal timing. It cannot say the V8 macro results proved that birds ignored new information, that their physical recourse was lost, that learned memory became maladaptive, or that resulting mismatch reduced fitness.

No frozen numerical result was modified. Updated manuscript only adjusts editorial interpretation and explicitly adds the previously unsupported mule-deer readiness-by-phase moderation endpoint from an existing post-freeze receipt.

## Next decisive evidence for a future ecology-first upgrade

A naturally or experimentally separated pre-decision information signal, independently measured feasible action and subsequent actual behavioral choice, with contemporary ecological cost/reproductive outcome and an explicit fixed-calendar/direct-local-forcing comparator. Until an admissible independent source exists, no new theory-only branch should be promoted as a high-impact natural result. Theoretical journal targeting remains The American Naturalist as in V8 publication decision.
