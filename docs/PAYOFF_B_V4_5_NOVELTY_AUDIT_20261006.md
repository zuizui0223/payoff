# PAYOFF-B V4.5 novelty audit — 2026-10-06

Status: **CURRENT NOVELTY BOUNDARY AFTER OBSERVABILITY AUDIT**

## Bottom line

V4.5 must not claim novelty for the general distinctions among:
- environmental correlation and prediction loss;
- environmental information and organismal cue use;
- information availability and information usage;
- feedforward and feedback;
- value of information;
- seasonal cue reliability;
- stagewise migration adjustment.

Those ideas all have substantial prior art.

## Key prior-art boundaries

### McNamara et al. 2011

Already formalizes timing decisions under incomplete and available cue
information, including cue-optimum correlation, slope, target variance and
fitness loss.

Therefore V4.5 does not introduce the idea that the biological meaning of a cue
depends on decision loss rather than correlation alone.

### Shaw & Couzin 2013

Already treats migration/residency and information usage in seasonal
environments.

Therefore V4.5 does not introduce the distinction between environmental
information structure and biological information use.

### Bergstrom & Lachmann / fitness-value-of-information literature

Already distinguishes statistical/information-theoretic content from
decision-theoretic or fitness value to an organism.

Therefore G_E versus G_O is an organizing notation, not a new information
theorem.

### Bernhardt et al. 2020

Feedforward and feedback are established complementary responses in variable
environments.

### Kölzsch et al. 2015 / Amaral et al. 2025

Stagewise environmental predictability, green-wave tracking and migration-speed
adjustment are prior empirical findings.

## What the new observability audit changes

The bird environmental variable is annual source-cell mid-green-up date.

In the restricted source-stage subset:
- source mid-green-up preceded source-front arrival in only 29.8% of early and
  45.8% of late annual observations;
- it occurred after target-front arrival in 41.6% and 36.1% of annual
  observations.

Therefore G_CV is not licensed as organismal information value.

The manuscript must distinguish:

    ideal-observer environmental forecastability G_E
        ->
    organismally accessible information G_O
        ->
    retained actionability r
        ->
    correction.

## Empirical contribution that survives

Within one multi-species eastern North American system, the analysis jointly
separates through time:

1. standardized source-target environmental coupling;
2. absolute target phenological variability;
3. held-out analyst forecast value of regional nonlocal environmental
   structure;
4. event ordering relative to population-front movement;
5. population arrival relative to target green-up;
6. population-level stage transformation in a restricted same-system subset.

The combined pattern is:

- target variability increased;
- regional nonlocal environmental forecastability increased strongly;
- the realized reconstructed source event was not consistently an online cue
  at the mapped source stage;
- target green-up advanced while population arrival changed little;
- route-level gains in analyst forecastability did not transfer into
  detectable bird-specific mismatch improvement;
- population-level timing nevertheless transformed across mapped stages in a
  restricted subset;
- individual mule deer independently demonstrate signed downstream correction.

The novelty is the **joint empirical decomposition of these layers**, not any
layer alone.

## Theoretical contribution that survives

The reduced seasonal architecture uses established decision theory but makes
the measurement hierarchy explicit:

    G_E(t)  = ideal-observer environmental forecast value
    G_O(t)  = organismally accessible decision value
    r(t)    = retained actionability
    N(t)    = r(t) G_O(t) - C(t).

For nested information sets, G_O <= G_E is prior decision-theoretic
monotonicity.

The candidate seasonal synthesis is:

> tracking fails at distinguishable interfaces: the environment may be
> forecastable without being biologically accessible, accessible information
> may arrive when response options are weak, and residual error may or may not
> be corrected downstream.

This is an ecological architecture, not a new generic information theorem.

## Same-system stagewise evidence

The 31-pair / 14-species bird subset provides a useful but secondary bridge:
- population front usually reaches source before target;
- front interval shortened;
- green-up interval changed little;
- source-to-target phase transformation changed;
- about 39% of the between-period source-stage phase shift was not retained at
  target on the aggregate mean scale.

But:
- the subset is selected toward shorter distances and stronger late coupling;
- measurement precision differs strongly between periods;
- inverse-variance weighting weakens the mean contrast;
- latent phase-retention slopes are not identifiable cleanly;
- the original Amaral study already established migration-speed response to
  green-up.

Therefore this is supporting stage geometry, not the novelty headline.

## Journal implication

### American Naturalist

Still credible and arguably cleaner than V4.4 because the paper no longer
confuses analyst predictability with organismal information.

Strengths:
- one ecological architecture;
- preregistered environmental coordinate;
- transparent posthoc diagnostic chain;
- explicit observability boundary;
- same-system population-stage bridge;
- independent individual-level correction anchor;
- strong prior-art discipline.

### Ecology Letters / Nature Ecology & Evolution / PNAS

Still not justified by current evidence.

A higher ceiling requires one natural system measuring, at several ordered
stages:

    external forecastability G_E
    + actual organismal cue access / inference G_O
    + correction opportunity r
    + behavioral response
    + phase outcome.

## Current one-sentence novelty

> **The study separates environmental forecastability from biological
> information access and downstream correction in the same seasonal-tracking
> framework, showing empirically that strong analyst forecast value need not
> identify the information actually available to a migrating organism.**
