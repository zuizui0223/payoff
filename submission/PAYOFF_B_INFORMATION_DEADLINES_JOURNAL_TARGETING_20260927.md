## First shot — Ecology Letters / Letter

The v2 manuscript now fits the Ecology Letters Letter envelope without
scientific compression:

- approximate main text: **3,816 words**;
- abstract: **150 words**;
- main display pieces: **6**;
- current references: 9, with full citation audit still pending.

Current Ecology Letters guidance allows up to 5,000 main-text words and six
figures/tables/text boxes for a Letter, with an abstract no longer than 150
words.

The stronger reason for the route change is scientific rather than numerical.
The v2 primary result is now a general ecological mechanism:

> **Improving a shared cue can transiently worsen coordination when interacting
> species cross their value-of-information thresholds at different cue
> qualities.**

This is followed by a second general result:

> **Network topology determines whether a transient information shock is erased
> or stored as a strict lower-payoff timing regime.**

The natural-data analyses act as discriminating tests and boundaries rather
than as one macroecological pattern that must carry the whole paper.

This architecture aligns more directly with a journal emphasizing concise,
hypothesis-driven and broadly novel ecological results than with a
macroecology-first framing.

### Editorial risk that must remain visible

The strongest hysteresis result is synthetic. No current natural dataset shows
the complete information-loss → partner-shift → information-recovery →
persistent-state sequence.

The broad-bird empirical result is pooled and dependence-sensitive.

The long-term cue-driver reversal test is negative.

These are not reasons to retune the science. They should be used in the cover
letter to show that the paper makes a sharply falsifiable theoretical claim and
reports the natural evidence ceiling transparently.

## Second route — Global Ecology and Biogeography / Research Article

GEB remains a coherent fallback, but it is no longer the natural first framing.

Current GEB guidance emphasizes broad-scale patterns in space, time or taxonomy
and general processes that underlie them. A Research Article is typically
around 5,000 main-body words, roughly 50 references and six to eight display
pieces, with a structured abstract up to 300 words.

A GEB submission would therefore need the **broad migratory-bird predictive
connectivity result** to move forward in the narrative, with the exact
information game presented as the process explaining a broad pattern.

That overlay is possible without changing any scientific result, but it would
make the paper less direct than the current v2.

## Novelty sentence for the cover letter

A concise provisional statement is:

> Existing theory shows that cue reliability matters and that acquiring
> information can be costly. We show a different consequence: when interacting
> species face unequal decision deadlines, monotonically improving the same cue
> can first desynchronize and then resynchronize their seasonal decisions, and
> interaction topology can determine whether the resulting transient mismatch
> disappears or persists as a lower-payoff historical state.

This statement is outcome-independent and must not change when the Aikens
Supplementary result is opened.
