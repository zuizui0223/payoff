# Cover letter — Theoretical Ecology

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Exactly one migration optimum under anti-phase environmental switching,”** for publication in *Theoretical Ecology*.

The paper asks a narrow but unresolved optimization question inside a canonical periodic metapopulation model. In the symmetric two-patch, two-season anti-phase system, explicit formulas show that intermediate migration can increase the long-run growth rate, but the existence of beneficial intermediate movement does not establish whether the growth premium has a unique optimum for every nonzero environmental contrast.

We prove that the exact temporal growth premium has exactly one stationary point on positive migration for every nonzero dimensionless contrast, and that this point is the unique global maximum. After nondimensionalization, all optima collapse to a single curve in environmental contrast. The optimal dimensionless migration approaches 1.606115… under weak contrast and 1 under strong contrast, linking the best movement rate directly to the environmental switching timescale.

The closed-form Floquet growth exponent itself is not claimed as new. Benaïm et al. (2023) give an explicit expression for the closely corresponding anti-phase switched system. The novelty here is deliberately narrower: the global uniqueness proof, the single scaling curve for the optimum, and its weak- and strong-contrast endpoint asymptotics. We also state explicitly that the theorem does not extend automatically to asymmetric, stochastic, multi-patch, or density-dependent systems.

We believe the manuscript fits *Theoretical Ecology* because it uses a compact mathematical result to answer an ecological question about connectivity in temporally alternating habitats, while keeping the model assumptions and generality boundary explicit. The analytic proof is accompanied by executable numerical verification, but the numerical receipt is treated only as an implementation audit and not as a substitute for the theorem.
