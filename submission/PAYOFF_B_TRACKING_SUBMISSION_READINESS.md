## Not required before a first manuscript freeze

The following are **not** prerequisites for freezing a submission draft:

- larger synthetic parameter sweeps;
- more stochastic replicates outside an identified robustness gap;
- additional taxa from the GEB programme;
- Aikens lambda outcome;
- empirical calibration of synthetic climate velocity;
- empirical calibration of the synthetic interaction strength;
- empirical corridor widths;
- a claim that the sampled barrier frequency estimates natural prevalence.

Adding these merely to make the simulation look larger would weaken the
claim boundary.

## Current stop rule for new simulation

A new synthetic run is justified only if it answers one of these:

1. a reviewer-relevant robustness concern not already covered by a frozen
   receipt;
2. a failure of one of the current figure/claim contracts;
3. a specific prior-art comparison requiring a matched model contrast.

Otherwise the programme advances through writing, figures and formal claim
discipline, not through more parameter volume.
