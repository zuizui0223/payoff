## First shot — Global Ecology and Biogeography, Research Article

Current GEB fit is strong because the paper's primary empirical generality test
is explicitly broad across taxa: 5,816 observations from 55 migratory bird
species, followed by mechanism decomposition across multiple migration systems.
The journal explicitly seeks broad-scale patterns in space, time and/or taxonomy
and the processes underlying them, with globally relevant conceptual
conclusions.

Current GEB Research Article envelope:

- roughly 5,000 main-body words;
- roughly 50 references;
- 6–8 display pieces;
- structured abstract <=300 words with:
  Aim; Location; Time period; Major taxa studied; Methods; Results; Main conclusions;
- double-anonymous review;
- 6–10 alphabetized keywords;
- short running title <40 characters;
- data and code accessible during review and archived in a stable public
  repository for publication;
- GitHub alone is not treated as a stable archive.

The integrated PREOUTCOME manuscript currently has:

- 3,458 machine-audited main-text words;
- 21 references;
- 6 main figures;
- 8 keywords;
- anonymous-text scan PASS.

Thus the journal-specific scientific rewrite is minimal. Required overlay work
is structural: structured abstract, running title, GEB figure legends,
double-anonymous data/code wording, and final stable reviewer link.

## Stretch alternative — Ecology Letters, Letter

The integrated paper also fits the numerical Letter envelope:

- main text <=5,000 words;
- <=6 figures/tables/text boxes.

However, the Letter abstract limit is <=150 words and the journal prioritizes
work with exceptionally high originality, general interest and urgency. The
integrated story can be adapted without new science, but the editorial pitch
would need to be much more compressed around one general ecological inference:

> low mismatch does not identify tracking architecture or remaining resilience.

Keep an Ecology Letters overlay as a transfer/stretched first-shot option only;
do not retune results to manufacture a stronger positive universal pattern.

## Broad global-change fallback — Global Change Biology

GCB accepts theoretical, modelling and observational work on biological
responses to environmental change and allows Research Articles up to 8,000
main-body words. It is structurally easy to fit, but the integrated manuscript
would need a more explicit global-change framing throughout. That reframing is
not required for the present GEB-first route.
