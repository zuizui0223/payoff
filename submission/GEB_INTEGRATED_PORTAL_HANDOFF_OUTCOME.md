## Remaining portal / author actions

After the outcome-rendered GEB audit passes, remaining blockers are external or
author-controlled:

1. create and verify the anonymized stable reviewer archive link;
2. populate final author order, affiliations, one corresponding author and
   institutional address/email;
3. confirm ORCID, funding, competing interests, acknowledgements, CRediT and
   relevant ethics/permissions statements;
4. confirm all-author approval and no simultaneous submission;
5. choose any conflict-checked suggested reviewers or handling editors;
6. upload through the live Wiley portal.

No scientific result may be changed during these actions.
