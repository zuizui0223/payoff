## Exact conditional Wright-Fisher benchmark
Hold A at frequency 1 and suppress mutation in B. With N copies and B's log fitness ratio s=beta_B*(s_B+J), P_ij=BinomialPMF(j;N, i*exp(s)/(N-i+i*exp(s))). For absorbing loss 0 and target >=h, Q is the transient matrix on 1..h-1 and r_i=sum_{j>=h}P_ij. Solve (I-Q)v=r; v_1 is the eventual probability of reaching target before loss from one copy. h=N gives fixation, h=ceil(.95*N) gives first 95% passage; not identical endpoints. At N=100, neutral fixation agrees with i/N to 1.45e-15. Across N=100,1000 and 12 existing payoff/intensity settings all 24 benchmarks pass row sums and linear residual checks (max residual 1.12e-15). Numerical exact finite-state probabilities up to floating point; no approximation by diffusion in these solves.

## Prethreshold loss with a fixed A trajectory
Externally impose p_A(t)=logistic(logit(.01)+s_A*t), suppress mutation and ignore B feedback in A. Let T be the first integer generation p_A>-s_B/J. In the Poisson rare-lineage approximation lambda_t=exp(beta_B*(s_B+J*p_A(t))). For survival to T, set v_T=1 and recurse v_t=1-exp(-lambda_t*v_{t+1}). For n0 initial copies, P_standing=1-(1-v_0)^n0 under independent lineages. Conditional on that A path and before threshold, every lambda_t is <=1 and increasing beta_B cannot improve the predicted survival probability. No theorem for feedback trajectories or finite-N drift outside this approximation.

## Time-limited late re-entry
Let F_h(u) be the single-copy first-passage CDF for target h by u generations before loss, under constant A=1 and no further mutations in the focal lineage. Compute it by propagating transient mass forward; early termination only when residual transient mass <1e-13. For independent Poisson mutation arrivals at mean N*mu per generation, starting with no B mutant, approximate P(at least one lineage reaches h by L) = 1-exp(-N*mu*sum_{u=0}^{L-1}F_h(u)). This is not the exact recurrent-mutation WF probability: neglect competing/overlapping lineages and back mutation. Compare simplified 1-exp(-N*mu*L*v_fix) which ignores sweep duration and uses a different target. At N=1000, qB=.6,k=.3, mu=1e-5, L=4000, beta=.25/1/4: time-aware 95%-hit approximation .05478/.20836/.67826, simplified fixation hazard .08829/.27312/.71715. Fixed-A/zero-B clock starts after A is fixed, so these cannot be directly equated to Wave 24 joint95 over the full 4000 generations.
