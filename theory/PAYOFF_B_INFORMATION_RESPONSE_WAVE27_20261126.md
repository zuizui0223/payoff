## Exact two-coordinate conditional problem

Let x_i in [0,1] be investment in information quality q_i=.5+a_i*x_i, a_i=q_max,i-.5; r_i in [0,1] is reaction strength; z_i=x_i*r_i. As in Wave 26, timing a(t)=.5+r_i*(S_i-.5), with S_i binary symmetric cue. Independent errors conditional on H give cue sign correlation 4*a_A*a_B*x_A*x_B. Each species receives expected squared-loss payoff
U_i=.75+k+a_i*z_i+J*z_i*z_j-D*r_i^2-E*r_j^2-c_I*x_i^2-c_R*r_i^p+v_i*x_i,
D=(1+k)/4, E=k/4, J=2*k*a_A*a_B, p=1 or 2. v_i*x_i is an explicitly assumed ancillary benefit of the same investment from a separate function, not an observed fitness effect. c_I=c_R=.06 preserves total .12 cost at x=r=1, v=0; centered fixed midpoint differs from historical anchored binary baseline. No independent sharing parameter is varied.

Exact potential equals sum_i(a_i*z_i-D*r_i^2-c_I*x_i^2-c_R*r_i^p+v_i*x_i)+J*z_A*z_B. Its unilateral changes equal those in own payoff, but it is not joint welfare. For fixed z_j, write A=a_i+J*z_j, d=D+c_R for p=2 or D for p=1, l=0 for p=2 or c_R for p=1. The own variable part is g(x,r)=A*x*r-d*r^2-l*r-c_I*x^2+v_i*x. Exact conditional best responses:
x*(r)=clip((A*r+v_i)/(2*c_I),0,1)
r*(x)=clip((A*x-l)/(2*d),0,1).
Maximize the profile g(x*(r),r) over r. For r below the saturation kink (2*c_I-v_i)/A, profile is (A*r+v_i)^2/(4*c_I)-d*r^2-l*r; above kink it is A*r+v_i-c_I-d*r^2-l*r. Evaluate endpoints, kink, admissible stationary points to obtain global joint best response. This is exact unilateral optimization conditional on partner product, not enumeration of all two-player equilibria. At r=0, x0=clip(v_i/(2*c_I),0,1). In particular costly information unused in seasonal timing cannot be maintained at the optimum by that timing benefit alone: if v_i=0 then x0=0. Positive x at r=0 requires another benefit (or a constraint not included here).

## Local barrier without discontinuous acquisition fees

For v=0 and linear response cost l>0, origin (x,r)=(0,0) is a strict local maximum of own payoff at fixed partner because near zero the term A*x*r cannot offset l*r and c_I*x^2. It is also a coordinate-wise global best response to itself: changing x alone or r alone is detrimental. Nevertheless a finite coordinated two-trait change can be beneficial. For quadratic response cost, origin has zero gradients; with A^2>4*c_I*d the Hessian has an improving direction into the positive quadrant and there is no strict local barrier, though an exactly-zero one-coordinate-at-a-time update remains trapped. For A^2<4*c_I*d the origin is the strict global conditional maximum at v=0. These separate a mutation-path constraint from a true payoff valley.

Exact witness A=.4, k=.5, v=0, p=1, z_j=0: d=.375, l=.06, c_I=.06. On unsaturated branch r<=.3, maximized profile relative to origin is h*r^2-l*r with h=A^2/(4*c_I)-d=.2916666667. Profile minimum at r*=l/(2h)=.1028571429, with x*=.3428571429 and valley depth l^2/(4h)=.0030857143. Every continuous path from origin to the joint optimum crosses this r* and cannot have payoff above the profile there; following x*(r) attains the bound. After r=l/h=.2057142857, the profile exceeds the original payoff, and joint optimum x=1,r=.4533333333 gains .0170666667. Therefore the minimum continuous-path payoff drop is exactly .0030857143 with partner held fixed. No fixed activation fee is needed; both costs are continuous. This is a conditional payoff valley, not an estimated population crossing time or proof of a particular genetic architecture.

With v>0, local profile slope at baseline r=0 is A*x0-l. For this witness the slope becomes positive for v>.018; at equality the lower-branch curvature is positive, so joint small steps are already favored. This is a local accessibility threshold, not a general threshold for biological information use. For B in the tested low-quality settings, v=.03 or .06 can sustain x_B=.25 or .5 while r_B=0 is still globally best even given A's evolved response.

## Numerical audit and equilibrium illustrations
32 settings (qmax B=.6/.8, k=.3/.5, p=1/2, v=.0/.03/.06/.12), 5 fixed partner products x_j*r_j=0/.25/.5/.75/1, 2 species => 320 unilateral optimization contexts. Exact conditional solution versus 3,264,320 grid evaluations (101x101 per context), maximum grid excess 2.78e-17; 5,184 individual payoff comparisons against eight explicitly enumerated state/cue outcomes, max error 4.44e-16; 5,184 unilateral potential identity checks, max error 3.33e-16. All 256 alternating update paths converged under the registered stopping rule. Joint-best-response convergence checked against exact unilateral regret <1e-9; coordinate-only results are not automatically Nash. No claim that the two starts and orders find every equilibrium.

Linear costs: 24/160 conditional contexts show strict local barrier plus positive finite joint deviation from inactive baseline. Quadratic costs: 0/160 meet that definition; 28/160 show a joint small-step improving direction despite zero baseline gradient. These are designed parameter counts, not prevalence. At v=0, all eight contexts trap the exactly-zero coordinate-only updates with positive joint deviation regret; four linear contexts have true local barrier for A, four quadratic contexts have improving joint directions for A. At joint best-response outcomes from baseline (A first), B's payoff is below its own baseline in 21/32 settings; summed payoff is below baseline in 2/32 settings (k=.5,p=1,v=0; A acts alone). Both costs are continuous. The sum is an assumed common-scale quantity.

At qmax B=.6,k=.5,p=1,v=.06, joint best responses converge to x_A=1,r_A=.453333; x_B=.5,r_B=0, q_A=.9,q_B=.55. Relative to the inactive seasonal-response baseline with the same ancillary benefit, changes (+.062067,-.025689), sum +.036378. Four of the 24 settings with v>0 show an inactive but positive-investment B from baseline; do not interpret these low-accuracy cues as perfect information or v as measured. At qmax B=.8,p=1,v=0 (both k levels), joint best response from inactive baseline vs fully active start reaches different Nash states: A-only vs both active. Order did not alter these four illustrated outcomes. This is equilibrium multiplicity in the sampled states, not a universal history-dependent transition or proof that natural populations use best-response updates.
